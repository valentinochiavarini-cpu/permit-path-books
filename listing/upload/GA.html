Product Description

Two parts, 20 road signs and 20 road rules, and you need 15 right on each.

Most people who fail don't fail for lack of effort. They read the Georgia manual once, then get caught on the exact numbers: Joshua's Law, the 40 hours with 6 at night, ADAP and the one year and one day on the permit. This book is built so that doesn't happen to you.

Learn a topic, then prove it. Each of the 13 chapters teaches one topic in plain English and ends with a 10-question quiz, every answer explained. Every Georgia rule is checked against Georgia DDS and Georgia law, and boxed wherever Georgia differs from other states.

Inside the book

  • A Georgia chapter on the whole path: the permit at 15, one year and one day on it, Joshua's Law (30 hours of class plus 6 of driving), 40 hours (6 at night) and ADAP
  • 80+ road signs and diagrams, with every color named
  • 130 chapter quiz questions with explanations
  • Full two-part mock exams: road signs and road rules
  • A road test chapter: what the examiner scores and the mistakes that fail people

Plus the free Permit Path practice app

  • 115 road signs in full color as flashcards
  • The full ebook on your phone, every sign in color

For teens, adults learning to drive for the first time, newcomers with a foreign license, and the parents helping them.

From the Author

We built this book because most people who fail the permit test don't fail for lack of effort. They read a long handbook once, then meet questions worded in ways they never practiced, and lose points on exact numbers. So we built it the other way around. Each chapter teaches one topic in plain words and ends with a quiz, so you find your gaps right away. Every Georgia rule was checked against the official manual and Georgia law. Then we connected the book to a free app that keeps testing you until your scores show you're ready. If you're the parent in the passenger seat, the road test chapter is there for you too. Read a chapter a day, take both mock exams under real conditions, and review every miss. Do that, and you'll walk in knowing what to expect.

Good luck on test day. The Permit Path team
