Product Description

A knowledge test at 80 percent, then a road sign test where you can miss only 3 of 12.

Most people who fail don't fail for lack of effort. They read the North Carolina manual once, then get caught on the exact numbers: the 60 hours, the 10 at night, the limit of 10 hours a week and the nine-month wait. This book is built so that doesn't happen to you.

Learn a topic, then prove it. Each of the 13 chapters teaches one topic in plain English and ends with a 10-question quiz, every answer explained. Every North Carolina rule is checked against NCDMV and North Carolina law, and boxed wherever North Carolina differs from other states.

Inside the book

  • A North Carolina chapter on the whole path: the Level 1 permit, the 60-hour driving log (10 at night, no more than 10 a week), nine months, then Levels 2 and 3
  • 80+ road signs and diagrams, with every color named
  • 130 chapter quiz questions with explanations
  • Full mock exams with a knowledge part and a road sign part
  • A road test chapter: what the examiner scores and the mistakes that fail people

Plus the free Permit Path practice app

The book teaches you. The app drills you until the answers are automatic. It opens in any browser: no download, no sign-up, no access code.

  • 600+ North Carolina questions by chapter, shuffled every time, each answer explained
  • A timed exam simulator at a full practice length
  • 115 road signs in full color as flashcards
  • The full ebook on your phone, every sign in color

For teens, adults learning to drive for the first time, newcomers with a foreign license, new residents who take the North Carolina road sign test, and the parents helping them.

From the Author

We built this book because most people who fail the permit test don't fail for lack of effort. They read a long handbook once, then meet questions worded in ways they never practiced, and lose points on exact numbers. So we built it the other way around. Each chapter teaches one topic in plain words and ends with a quiz, so you find your gaps right away. Every North Carolina rule was checked against the official manual and North Carolina law. Then we connected the book to a free app that keeps testing you until your scores show you're ready. If you're the parent in the passenger seat, the road test chapter is there for you too. Read a chapter a day, take both mock exams under real conditions, and review every miss. Do that, and you'll walk in knowing what to expect.

Good luck on test day. The Permit Path team
