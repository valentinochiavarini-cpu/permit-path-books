Product Description

Segment 1, Segment 2, then the road test: Michigan has more steps than most states.

Most people who fail don't fail for lack of effort. They read the Michigan manual once, then get caught on the exact numbers: the 30 hours (2 at night) to start Segment 2 and the 50 hours (10 at night) before the road test. This book is built so that doesn't happen to you.

Learn a topic, then prove it. Each of the 13 chapters teaches one topic in plain English and ends with a 10-question quiz, every answer explained. Every Michigan rule is checked against the Michigan Secretary of State and Michigan law, and boxed wherever Michigan differs from other states.

Inside the book

  • A Michigan chapter on the whole path: Level 1 and Segment 1, the 30 hours (2 at night) for Segment 2, 50 hours (10 at night) before the road test and the Level 2 limits
  • 80+ road signs and diagrams, with every color named
  • 130 chapter quiz questions with explanations
  • Full two-part mock exams: road rules and road signs
  • A road test chapter: what the examiner scores and the mistakes that fail people

Plus the free Permit Path practice app

  • A timed exam simulator at a full practice length
  • 115 road signs in full color as flashcards
  • The full ebook on your phone, every sign in color

For teens, adults learning to drive for the first time, newcomers with a foreign license, and the parents helping them.

From the Author

We built this book because most people who fail the permit test don't fail for lack of effort. They read a long handbook once, then meet questions worded in ways they never practiced, and lose points on exact numbers. So we built it the other way around. Each chapter teaches one topic in plain words and ends with a quiz, so you find your gaps right away. Every Michigan rule was checked against the official manual and Michigan law. Then we connected the book to a free app that keeps testing you until your scores show you're ready. If you're the parent in the passenger seat, the road test chapter is there for you too. Read a chapter a day, take both mock exams under real conditions, and review every miss. Do that, and you'll walk in knowing what to expect.

Good luck on test day. The Permit Path team
