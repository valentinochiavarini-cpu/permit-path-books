Product Description

50 questions, 40 to pass, and a one-week wait if you miss.

Most people who fail don't fail for lack of effort. They read the New Jersey manual once, then get caught on the exact numbers: the six months and the 50 hours with 10 in the dark, both required, plus the GDL limits. This book is built so that doesn't happen to you.

Learn a topic, then prove it. Each of the 13 chapters teaches one topic in plain English and ends with a 10-question quiz, every answer explained. Every New Jersey rule is checked against the New Jersey MVC and New Jersey law, and boxed wherever New Jersey differs from other states.

From the Author

We built this book because most people who fail the permit test don't fail for lack of effort. They read a long handbook once, then meet questions worded in ways they never practiced, and lose points on exact numbers. So we built it the other way around. Each chapter teaches one topic in plain words and ends with a quiz, so you find your gaps right away. Every New Jersey rule was checked against the official manual and New Jersey law. Then we connected the book to a free app that keeps testing you until your scores show you're ready. If you're the parent in the passenger seat, the road test chapter is there for you too. Read a chapter a day, take both mock exams under real conditions, and review every miss. Do that, and you'll walk in knowing what to expect.

Good luck on test day. The Permit Path team
