Product Description

You can study the handbook and still fail the New Jersey permit test.

The New Jersey knowledge test asks 50 questions, and you need 40 right. Plenty of smart people fail it. Not because the rules are hard, but because the handbook never quizzes you, and the exam wants exact answers: how many feet, which hours, what limit.

This book teaches you, then tests you, until you're ready.

Most permit books are question dumps. This one teaches each topic in plain words, then quizzes you on it right away, with every answer explained. New Jersey rules are checked against the New Jersey MVC and New Jersey law. You get 80+ road signs and diagrams, 130 quiz questions, full 50-question mock exams, a road test chapter and a supervised driving log.

Ready in two weeks: one chapter a day, then the mock exams.

From the Author

We built this book because most people who fail the permit test don't fail for lack of effort. They read a long handbook once, then meet questions worded in ways they never practiced, and lose points on exact numbers. So we built it the other way around. Each chapter teaches one topic in plain words and ends with a quiz, so you find your gaps right away. Every New Jersey rule was checked against the official manual and New Jersey law. Then we connected the book to a free app that keeps testing you until your scores show you're ready. If you're the parent in the passenger seat, the road test chapter and driving log are there for you too. Read a chapter a day, take both mock exams under real conditions, and review every miss. Do that, and you'll walk in knowing what to expect.

Good luck on test day. The Permit Path team
