Product Description

40 questions, 30 to pass. That's the Ohio temps test.

Most people who fail don't fail for lack of effort. They read the Ohio manual once, then get caught on the exact numbers: the 24 classroom hours, the 50 hours with 10 at night and the six months on the permit. This book is built so that doesn't happen to you.

Learn a topic, then prove it. Each of the 13 chapters teaches one topic in plain English and ends with a 10-question quiz, every answer explained. Every Ohio rule is checked against the Ohio BMV and Ohio law, and boxed wherever Ohio differs from other states.

Inside the book

  • An Ohio chapter on the whole path: the temps permit, 24 hours of class plus 8 behind the wheel, 50 hours (10 at night) and six months before the road test
  • 80+ road signs and diagrams, with every color named
  • 130 chapter quiz questions with explanations
  • Full 40-question mock exams
  • A road test chapter: what the examiner scores and the mistakes that fail people

Plus the free Permit Path practice app

The book teaches you. The app drills you until the answers are automatic. It opens in any browser: no download, no sign-up, no access code.

  • 600+ Ohio questions by chapter, shuffled every time, each answer explained
  • A timed exam simulator with the real test's length and passing score
  • 115 road signs in full color as flashcards
  • The full ebook on your phone, every sign in color

For teens, adults learning to drive for the first time, newcomers with a foreign license, and the parents helping them.

From the Author

We built this book because most people who fail the permit test don't fail for lack of effort. They read a long handbook once, then meet questions worded in ways they never practiced, and lose points on exact numbers. So we built it the other way around. Each chapter teaches one topic in plain words and ends with a quiz, so you find your gaps right away. Every Ohio rule was checked against the official manual and Ohio law. Then we connected the book to a free app that keeps testing you until your scores show you're ready. If you're the parent in the passenger seat, the road test chapter is there for you too. Read a chapter a day, take both mock exams under real conditions, and review every miss. Do that, and you'll walk in knowing what to expect.

Good luck on test day. The Permit Path team
