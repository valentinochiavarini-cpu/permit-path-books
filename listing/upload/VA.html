Product Description

You can study the handbook and still fail the Virginia permit test.

The Virginia knowledge exam starts with 10 road signs, and you must get all 10 right. Then come 30 general questions, with 24 needed to pass. Plenty of smart people fail it. Not because the rules are hard, but because the handbook never quizzes you, and the exam wants exact answers: how many feet, which hours, what limit.

This book teaches you, then tests you, until you're ready.

Most permit books are question dumps. This one teaches each topic in plain words, then quizzes you on it right away, with every answer explained. Virginia rules are checked against the Virginia DMV and Virginia law. You get 80+ road signs and diagrams, 130 quiz questions, full two-part mock exams (road signs and general knowledge) and a road test chapter.

Ready in two weeks: one chapter a day, then the mock exams.

Plus a free practice app

  • 115 road signs in full color as flip cards

For teens, adults learning to drive for the first time, newcomers with a foreign license, and the parents helping them.

Pass the mock exams and a few timed runs in the app, and you'll walk in knowing you're ready.

From the Author

We built this book because most people who fail the permit test don't fail for lack of effort. They read a long handbook once, then meet questions worded in ways they never practiced, and lose points on exact numbers. So we built it the other way around. Each chapter teaches one topic in plain words and ends with a quiz, so you find your gaps right away. Every Virginia rule was checked against the official manual and Virginia law. Then we connected the book to a free app that keeps testing you until your scores show you're ready. If you're the parent in the passenger seat, the road test chapter is there for you too. Read a chapter a day, take both mock exams under real conditions, and review every miss. Do that, and you'll walk in knowing what to expect.

Good luck on test day. The Permit Path team
