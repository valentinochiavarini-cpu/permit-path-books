Product Description

20 questions, 14 to pass, and at least 2 of the 4 road sign questions must be right.

Most people who fail don't fail for lack of effort. They read the New York manual once, then get caught on the exact numbers: the 50 hours with 15 after dark, the 5-hour course and junior license limits. This book is built so that doesn't happen to you.

Learn a topic, then prove it. Each of the 13 chapters teaches one topic in plain English and ends with a 10-question quiz, every answer explained. Every New York rule is checked against the New York DMV and New York law, and boxed wherever New York differs from other states.

From the Author

We built this book because most people who fail the permit test don't fail for lack of effort. They read a long handbook once, then meet questions worded in ways they never practiced, and lose points on exact numbers. So we built it the other way around. Each chapter teaches one topic in plain words and ends with a quiz, so you find your gaps right away. Every New York rule was checked against the official manual and New York law. Then we connected the book to a free app that keeps testing you until your scores show you're ready. If you're the parent in the passenger seat, the road test chapter is there for you too. Read a chapter a day, take both mock exams under real conditions, and review every miss. Do that, and you'll walk in knowing what to expect.

Good luck on test day. The Permit Path team
