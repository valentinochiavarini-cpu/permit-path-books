# How to Use This Book

You are about to take two tests: a written knowledge test, usually on a screen at a licensing office, and later a road test with an examiner in the passenger seat. This book gets you ready for both. It is built around one idea: **learn a topic, then test yourself on it right away.**

## What is inside

- **Chapter 1** covers the {{STATE}} licensing process: who can get a permit, what to bring, what the knowledge test looks like and the rules you follow as a new driver.
- **Chapters 2 to 12** teach the rules of the road. Boxes marked **{{STATE}} rule** give you the exact {{STATE}} figure where states differ.
- **Every chapter ends with a 10-question quiz.** The answers, each with a short explanation, are in the Answer Key at the back of the book.
- **Chapter 13** is about test day: how to study the last week, and what the road test examiner is watching for.
- **Two full mock exams** built like the real {{STATE}} test (the page before Mock Exam 1 explains how they are sized). Take them only after you finish the chapters, and time yourself.
- **A supervised driving log** at the back, to record the practice hours a learner needs.

## How to study

1. Read one chapter at a time. Most take 30 to 45 minutes.
2. Take the chapter quiz without looking back. Then check your answers.
3. For every miss, reread the section the explanation points to. Misses are where your points are hiding.
4. Practice the same chapter online until you score 90 percent or better.
5. When every chapter is done, take Mock Exam 1. Review every miss. Take Mock Exam 2 a few days later.

> **Road tip:** The book is printed in black and white to keep it affordable. Road signs are learned partly by color, so every color is named in the text, and the online flashcards show every sign in full color.
