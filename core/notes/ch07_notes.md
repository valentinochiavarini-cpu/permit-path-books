# Chapter 7 notes: Lanes, Passing and Highway Driving

Written 2026-10-27.

## Facts verified
- Lane use: left lane to pass or turn left, right lane to enter or exit; stay in one lane; do not weave; change lanes: signal, mirrors, look over shoulder; cross multiple lanes one at a time. California Driver Handbook, Navigating the Roads: https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook/california-driver-handbook/navigating-the-roads/
- Freeway entering: be at or near traffic speed, merge, stop only if absolutely necessary, highway traffic has the right of way; exiting: signal about five seconds before, change lanes one at a time, exit at safe speed, do not cross solid lines. Same CA page.
- HOV lanes marked with a diamond and signs; occupancy and hours on signs; buses, motorcycles (unless posted) and decal clean-air vehicles; do not cross double solid lines, use designated openings. Same CA page.
- Passing rules: pass on left; pass on right only when the vehicle ahead turns left or on roads with two or more lanes each way or one-way multi-lane roads; never on shoulder unless signed or directed; do not exceed the limit to pass; no passing near curves or hill crests without view, within 100 feet of railroad crossings, bridges, tunnels or viaducts with obstructed view; see the passed car's front in the inside mirror before returning; being passed: slightly decrease speed and stay centered; if many vehicles pass on the right, move right. New York State Driver's Manual, Chapter 6: https://dmv.ny.gov/new-york-state-drivers-manual-practice-tests/chapter-6-passing
- Double solid white lane line = crossing prohibited (MUTCD 11th ed., Section 3B.06 para 12); broken white = crossing allowed (3B.06 para 5); dotted white line separates acceleration and deceleration lanes (3B.07). https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/part3.pdf
- HOV diamond symbol used only for HOV lanes; HOV 2+ notation; EXPRESS LANE(S) for priced managed lanes: MUTCD 11th ed., Sections 2G.03, 2G.05, 2G.10, 2G.17. https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/Chapter2g.pdf
- Purple for toll account only lanes: MUTCD Chapter 2F (see ch02 notes).
- Freeway breakdown: completely off the pavement, stay inside with doors locked until help arrives (CA): https://dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook/california-driver-handbook/navigating-the-roads-cont1/

## Facts from general knowledge, primary page not fetched (confirm in fact check)
- "See both headlights in your inside mirror" before returning: widely taught; NY says see the passed car's front bumper. Equivalent meaning.
- Unlawful to speed up while being passed: Uniform Vehicle Code 11-303 and most state codes.
- 100 foot signal before lane change or turn: common (NY, CA), written as "many states".
- Highway hypnosis and breaks every two hours: common manual and safety guidance.
- Pavement diamond color white: MUTCD 3B.20 says markings are white unless stated otherwise; Section 3E text not fetched.

## Rules that vary by state (covered by inserts)
- HOV occupancy, hours, clean-air decal and motorcycle access, fines, express and toll lane rules: `hov`.
- Left lane camping laws, signal distance and passing distance limits vary by state; the chapter words these as "most states" or "many states". Move Over: covered in Chapter 4 (`move_over`).

## Uncertain
- Whether to stay in the car after a breakdown is advice, not law; FHWA and AAA guidance agree it is usually safer; chapter words it as "in most breakdowns".
