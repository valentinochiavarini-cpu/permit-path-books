# Chapter 12 notes: Your Car, Seat Belts and Passengers

Written 2026-10-27.

## Facts verified
- Seat belt fit: lap belt across hips not stomach, shoulder belt across middle of chest away from neck, never behind back or under arm; airbags work with belts; pregnancy fit; ejection almost always deadly: https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/seat-belts
- Airbags: 10 inch minimum from breastbone to cover; children under 13 in back; rear-facing seats not in front of an active airbag: https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/air-bags
- Car seat stages (rear-facing as long as possible to seat limits, forward-facing with harness and tether, booster until belt fits, lap belt on upper thighs, shoulder belt on chest and shoulder, back seat through age 12): https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/car-seats-and-booster-seats
- Heatstroke: child body heats 3 to 5 times faster than adult; windows cracked or shade does little; over 50 percent of deaths from a forgotten child; never leave a child alone; lock the car; call 911: https://www.nhtsa.gov/campaign/heatstroke
- New Hampshire is the only state without an adult seat belt law; about 34 or 35 states primary, 15 secondary: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seat_belt_laws_in_the_United_States (secondary source; IIHS and GHSA pages blocked this session). Chapter gives no count.

## From general knowledge, not fetched this session (confirm in fact check)
- Tire pressure on driver door placard, sidewall number is the maximum, check cold; tread wear bars; penny test (Lincoln's head visible = about 2/32 inch, replace): NHTSA tire page https://www.nhtsa.gov/equipment/tires
- Dashboard light color convention (red urgent, yellow caution, green/blue info) and symbol shapes: general owner's manual convention, ISO 2575 symbols. Phrased as "usually" and points readers to the owner's manual.
- Head restraint middle level with top of ears: IIHS guidance.
- Side mirror setting (barely see own car at inner edge): matches most state manuals.
- "Car seat that moves more than an inch at the belt path is too loose": NHTSA/AAP installation guidance.
- Liability insurance required in nearly every state (New Hampshire is the long-standing exception; Virginia ended its uninsured motorist fee option in 2024). Chapter says "nearly every state".
- Electronic proof of insurance accepted in nearly all states (chapter says "in most states").
