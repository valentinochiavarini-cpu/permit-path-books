# Chapter 13 notes: Test Day

Written 2026-10-27. Official state road test pages used only to find what is common across states.

## Facts verified
- California DMV, Behind the Wheel Drive Test Preparation (FFDL 22): https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/file/ffdl-22-pdf
  Pre-drive vehicle check (plates, signals, brake lights, horn, tires, brakes, driver window, windshield, mirrors, doors, passenger seat, seat belts, parking brake); locate headlights, wipers, defroster, flashers; demonstrate parking brake; proof of insurance or test postponed; test rescheduled if vehicle fails; maneuvers: turns, stops, straight-line backing, lane changes, street traffic.
- California DMV, Driving Test Criteria (DL 955): https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/file/driving-test-criteria-dl-955-pdf
  Critical driving errors: examiner intervention; striking objects or curb/sidewalk; disobeying signs and signals (rolling stop, unnecessary stop at green, lane markings); passing stopped school bus, not yielding to emergency vehicles; causing evasive action; no head/mirror checks for lane change, merge, backing, pulling from curb; blocking intersection; speed; auxiliary equipment; wrong lane turns. Backing: turn and look through rear window, cameras may not show all hazards. Signal before turns, stop behind limit line.
- PennDOT Driver's Manual, Testing: https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dmv/driver-services/pennsylvania-drivers-manual/online-drivers-manual/testing
  Originals required; vehicle check (registration, insurance, inspection, lights, horn, brakes, wipers, signals, mirrors, doors, seats, tires, belts); controls demonstration; self-parking features must be off; failure causes (controls, crash, traffic law violation, not following instructions, no signals, dangerous driving); retest wait and attempt limits per permit.
- Texas, 37 Tex. Admin. Code 15.56: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/texas/37-Tex-Admin-Code-SS-15-56
  Road test = on-street, backing, parallel parking (if applicable); disqualification for preventable contact, wheels over curb or onto sidewalk, loss of control, violation of law, too many deductions, refusal to try a maneuver.
- New York DMV, Road Test Results: https://dmv.ny.gov/driver-license/road-test-results and Prepare for your road test: https://dmv.ny.gov/driver-license/prepare-your-road-test
  Scored categories (leaving curb, intersections, turn width, parking/backing/3-point turn, lane use, following distance, speed, vehicle control); physical permit and originals; only accompanying driver may ride; arrive early; wait to retest; permit valid until expiration.
- Minnesota DPS road test blog: https://dps.mn.gov/blog/Pages/20240909-road-test.aspx
  Controls check, only applicant and examiner in vehicle, temporary paper license on passing.
