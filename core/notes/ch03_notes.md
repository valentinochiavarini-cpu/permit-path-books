# Chapter 3 notes: Signals, Lights and Pavement Markings

Written 2026-10-27.

## Facts verified (FHWA MUTCD 11th Edition, December 2023)
- Steady green, green arrow, steady yellow, yellow arrow, steady red, red arrow meanings: Section 4A.03. https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/part4.pdf
- Flashing yellow, flashing yellow arrow, flashing red (stop sign rules), flashing red arrow; flashing green has no meaning and shall not be used: Section 4A.04. Same PDF.
- Pedestrian WALK (walking person), flashing upraised hand (do not start, finish crossing), steady upraised hand; flashing WALK not used: Section 4A.06. Same PDF.
- Ramp meters exist as freeway entrance ramp control signals (Section 4A.01 list). Detail section not reached in fetch.
- Colors of lines: yellow = opposite directions, left edge of divided highways, one way roads and ramps, two way left turn and reversible lanes; white = same direction and right edge (3A.03). Solid discourages or prohibits crossing, broken permits, double = maximum restriction, dotted = extension or lane drop warning (3A.04). Center lines yellow, single solid yellow not used as center line on two way road; four or more lane undivided roads use double solid yellow (3B.01). Lane lines white, double solid white prohibits crossing (3B.06). Edge lines (3B.09). Two way left turn lane: broken inside, solid outside, white two way arrows (3B.05). Reversible lanes: double broken yellow, with signs or lane use signals (3B.04). Stop lines solid white; yield lines white triangles pointing toward approaching traffic (3B.19). Crosswalks white, high visibility patterns (3C.03 to 3C.08). Word, arrow and ONLY markings white (3B.20 to 3B.24). https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/part3.pdf
- RXR pavement marking: Section 8C.02. https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/part8.pdf
- Lane use control signals (green down arrow, steady yellow X, steady red X, white two way left turn arrow): MUTCD 2009 Section 4M.02 (https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/htm/2009/part4/part4m.htm). Flashing yellow X = lane may be used only for a left turn: New York State Driver's Manual, Chapter 4 (https://dmv.ny.gov/new-york-state-drivers-manual-practice-tests/chapter-4-traffic-control). The 11th Edition section number for lane use control signals was not reached (PDF text truncated); confirm in the fact check.
- Dark signal = all way stop: New York manual Chapter 4; California Driver Handbook, Laws and Rules of the Road (https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook/california-driver-handbook/laws-and-rules-of-the-road/).
- Traffic officers override signals, signs and markings: New York manual Chapter 4.
- Double solid yellow may be crossed to turn left into or out of a driveway or road: New York manual Chapter 4. California also allows U-turns across it; written as "some states" in the text.
- Pedestrian countdown shows seconds left to cross: California handbook.

## Rules that vary by state
- Turn on red, left turn on red from one way to one way, red arrow turns, city bans (for example New York City bans turn on red unless a sign allows it): `turn_on_red`. The text says every state allows right on red after a stop unless prohibited, and "most states" for left on red one way to one way. The red arrow rule is worded as "states differ"; the insert should state whether a turn on a red arrow is ever allowed.
- U-turn across double yellow: varies; Chapter 5 `u_turn` insert.
