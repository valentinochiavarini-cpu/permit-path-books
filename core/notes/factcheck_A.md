## Changes to chapter text

| File | Old | New | Reason and source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ch02 intro | "Every state follows the same federal manual for signs, signals and road markings," | "Every state follows the federal manual for signs, signals and road markings, or its own version that must closely match it," | Some states (for example California) adopt their own state MUTCD, which must be in substantial conformance with the national MUTCD (23 CFR 655.603). The old line was stated as universal. |
| ch02 2.2 Remember it | "The more sides a sign has, the more it asks of you. The octagon has the most sides and demands a full stop." | "The octagon is the only eight-sided sign, and it always demands a full stop." | The old rule is false as stated: a pentagon (5 sides) and a diamond (4 sides) are warnings, while the 3-sided yield triangle asks more of you than either. Minimal fix to the first two sentences; the circle sentence kept. |
| ch02 2.6 | "An **End School Zone** sign marks where the zone ends." | "An **End School Zone** sign, where one is posted, marks where the zone ends." | S5-2 is optional (MUTCD 7B.02 para 07). |
| ch02 2.7 | "Most work zone crashes are rear end crashes, so leave extra room" | "Rear end crashes are a large share of work zone crashes, so leave extra room" | FHWA facts and stats (21 to 23 percent of fatal work zone crashes). |
| ch02 2.7 Arrow Boards | "...is the **caution mode**: work is near the road, but you do not need to change lanes. Slow down and stay alert." | "...is the **caution mode**: work is on or near the shoulder or road ahead, and no lane merge is shown. Slow down, stay alert and follow any other signs or flaggers." | Caution mode is also used when one lane of a two-lane two-way road is closed (2009 MUTCD 6F.61; 11th ed. 6L.06). |
| ch06 6.2 | "The number shown on the electronic sign is the legal limit at that moment." | "When the electronic sign reads SPEED LIMIT, the number shown is the legal limit at that moment." | Some electronic signs show advisory speeds, not regulatory limits; only a variable Speed Limit sign is enforceable. |
| ch06 6.4 | "these two parts together take one to two seconds." | "these two parts together take about one and a half to two and a half seconds, and longer when you are tired or distracted." | Virginia Code 46.2-880 (1.5 s); Georgia DDS 2.6 (about 2.5 s). |
| ch07 7.6 | "A **freeway** (also called an expressway or interstate) is a divided highway with no cross traffic." | "A **freeway** (most interstates are freeways) is a divided highway with no cross traffic." | MUTCD defines an expressway as a divided highway with partial control of access, so it can have at-grade crossings; "expressway" is not a synonym of freeway. |
| ch08 8.6 | "Some places are off limits for parking in every state," | "Some places are off limits for parking in almost every state," | The list includes items (bridge, overpass, left side of a two way road) not banned identically in every state; core must not state variable rules as universal. |
| ch08 8.6 | "**No Parking:** you may stop briefly to load or unload people or things, but you may not leave the car." | "**No Parking:** you may stop only while you load or unload people or things." | "May not leave the car" is the NY No Standing rule, and loading things usually means leaving the car. NY Driver's Manual Ch. 7. |

## Unresolved

1. MUTCD 11th edition Chapters 6K, 6L and 3E.03 could not be read (proxy blocks the PDF host; page fetch truncates Part 6 at 6H and Part 3 inside 3E.02). Rules were confirmed in the 2009 edition. The C02-026 citation "Section 6L.06" rests on a secondary source. Recheck against https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/part6.pdf when a full download is possible.
2. Interstate shield colors (red top, blue below, white numerals) are not in the MUTCD text; they are in Standard Highway Signs, which was not fetched. Widely known and almost certainly right.
3. No Passing Zone pennant placement on the left side (2C.53 body text) could not be read in the fetch; this is long-standing MUTCD rule. Left as is.
4. Colors for One Way (white arrow on black) and Wrong Way (white on red) are in Standard Highway Signs, not the 2B text read. Standard and not doubted.
5. ch07 "Freeways are the safest roads per mile driven" is a claim with no source logged. FHWA data show Interstates have the lowest fatality rate per mile traveled; consider citing or softening to "among the safest".
6. ch02 2.2 Remember it: the remaining sentence about the circle ("gives you the earliest warning, because a train cannot stop for you") is a teaching device, not a rule. Left.
7. `ch02_notes.md` lists Two Way Traffic as 2C.21 (should be 2C.51). Notes file left for the writer.
8. ch06 "Most states make it illegal to drive so slowly that you block the normal and reasonable flow of traffic": UVC 11-805 and most state codes; not checked state by state.
