# Chapter 2 notes: Road Signs

Written 2026-10-27. Model chapter for the core.

## Facts verified (FHWA MUTCD 11th Edition, December 2023)
Source index: https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/kno_11th_Edition.htm (note: 11th Edition Revision 1, December 2025, is now current; nothing in this chapter depends on a Revision 1 change as far as checked).

- Reserved shapes (Section 2A.05, Table 2A-1): octagon = Stop; downward triangle = Yield; circle = Grade Crossing Advance Warning (W10-1); pennant = No Passing Zone (W14-3); pentagon = School (S1-1) and County Route (M1-6); crossbuck = Grade Crossing (R15-1). Diamond = warning series; rectangle = regulatory and guide. https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/Chapter2a.pdf
- Colors (Section 2A.06, Table 2A-2): coral and light blue reserved for future use; purple restricted per Chapter 2F (toll); red only for stop, yield, prohibition elements. Same PDF.
- Signs on right side of roadway (Section 2A.13); retroreflective or illuminated (Section 2A.21).
- Regulatory signs rectangular unless designated otherwise (Section 2B.02); ALL WAY plaque white on red (2B.04); Speed Limit black on white (2B.21); lane control and ONLY (2B.27, 2B.28). https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/Chapter2b.pdf
- Warning signs yellow diamonds, black legend (Section 2C.02); pedestrian, bicycle and playground may be fluorescent yellow-green; school signs fluorescent yellow-green. Chevrons on outside of curve (2C.08); Merge vs Lane Ends (2C.45, 2C.47); Divided Highway and Two Way Traffic (2C.20, 2C.21); Low Clearance (2C.25); Slippery When Wet (2C.30); advisory speed plaque (2C.59). https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/Chapter2c.pdf
- School (Part 7, Sections 7B.01 to 7B.05, 7D.02): S1-1 fluorescent yellow-green; school crossing = S1-1 + diagonal downward arrow W16-7P; School Speed Limit assembly = SCHOOL plaque + R2-1 + WHEN CHILDREN ARE PRESENT / WHEN FLASHING / times plaque; END SCHOOL ZONE S5-2; crossing guards stand in the roadway, STOP paddle, all vehicles must stop. https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/part7.pdf
- Work zones (Part 6): TTC warning signs orange diamonds, black legend (6H.01); incident signs may be fluorescent pink (6H.01); ROAD WORK AHEAD first advance sign (6H.02); STOP/SLOW paddle octagonal (6D.02); flagger procedures (6D.05); Flagger sign W20-7 and BE PREPARED TO STOP W3-4 (6H.15). https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/part6.pdf
