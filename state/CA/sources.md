All checked 2026-10-28. "HB" is the California Driver's Handbook (DL 600, copyright 2025, CC BY-NC 4.0), read from the Drive copy (file ID 1qT23zBRVYqr3VH6228kwmf7OSUMlBWCF). The online handbook at dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook (Section 3 last modified 2025-06-19) matches it; no newer edition found. ## Not used or unresolved
- Knowledge test question count: not published on any current DMV page. Secondary sources cite 46 questions (38 to pass) for minors and 36 (30 to pass) for adults; not used. A 2019 DMV fast fact (cited by passrates.uk) lets provisional applicants miss up to 8.
- At-home online knowledge test (2022 to 2024): per passrates.uk, ended for original applicants on 2025-01-03; current DMV pages list online testing only for renewals, which is what the book says.
- Drive retest fee: FEES and TEEN say $9; the DL page still says $7. Book uses $9.
- First DUI conviction suspension: HB Section 9 says DMV suspends or revokes for one year; VC 13352(a)(1) says six months for a first conviction. Insert follows the statute; no bank question on this figure.
- AB 366 (2026) ignition interlock: press reports say it extends the program; VC 23575.3 keeps IID discretionary for a first non-injury DUI, so the insert says a court may order one.
- AB 382 statewide 20 mph school zones from a later date: left out (no year in the book).
- New residents' drive test: DL page lists none for a valid out-of-state license but does not state a waiver in words.
- Vehicle registration deadline for new residents: not verified, left out.
- **Question count (mocks)**: DMV research report RSS-06-221 (dmv.ca.gov, Dec 2006): 46 questions under 18 (8 errors allowed), 36 for 18+ (6 errors), 18 renewal. FFDL 19 (rev. 7/2019): minors may miss no more than 8. Current pages give no count. Mocks use 46, 38 to pass (owner audience is teens). Checked 2026-10-28.
