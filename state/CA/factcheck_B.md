## Verified (no change)

- **AB 390 Move Over** (eff. 2026-01-01): VC 21809(a) covers stationary emergency vehicles, tow trucks and marked maintenance vehicles with amber lights, and any stationary vehicle with hazard lights or warning devices (cones, flares); change lanes if practicable, else slow to a reasonable and prudent speed. Insert and CA-056, CA-057 correct. 300 ft behind emergency vehicle: HB Section 7 (CA-058).
- **AB 382 school zones**: DMV 2026 and https://www.bwslaw.com/insights/ab-382-statewide-20-miles-per-hour-school-zones-and-local-options/ : local 20 mph option now (VC 22352.5), with sign conditions WHEN FLASHING, CHILDREN ARE PRESENT or hours; statewide default becomes 20 mph on 2031-01-01; 15 mph residential option kept (VC 22358.4). Current VC 22352(b)(2) still 25 mph within 500 ft. Insert, CA-045, CA-046 correct.
- **Clean air decals**: invalid from 2025-10-01 (DMV CAV page). Insert and CA-086 correct. HB Section 6 still lists decals; the insert overrides it, correctly.
- **Daylighting**: VC 22500(n)(1)(A) 20 ft approach side, 15 ft with curb extension. Hydrant 15 ft (VC 22514), fire station 15 ft (22500(d)), ramp 3 ft and 18 in curb (HB Section 6), rail 7.5 ft (VC 22521).
- **Insurance 30/60/15** (SB 1107, from 2025-01-01): CA-132, CA-133 correct.
- **AB 366**: extends the interlock program sunset to 2033-01-01 (DMV 2026). VC 23575.3(h)(1) keeps an IID discretionary for a first non-injury DUI (court may order, or restricted license with IID). Insert "A court may order an ignition interlock device" is correct. Press items (KTVU) claiming a mandate for all first offenders conflict with the statute text; not used.
- **First DUI suspension**: VC 13352(a)(1)(A): "suspended for a period of six months" (operative 2019, in effect until 2033). HB Section 9 says "DMV will suspend or revoke your driving privilege for one year" for any DUI conviction, a simplification that is wrong for a first offense. The DUI page gives no conviction length. Resolved: insert keeps 6 months (statute governs). No bank question tests this figure, so a test taker is not exposed to the HB conflict through this book.
- **APS and refusal**: DUI page: 0.08 first offense four months; refusal 1 year, 2-year revocation (second in 10 years), 3-year (third); under 21 at 0.01 one year; 30-day temporary license; hearing within 10 days (HB says from arrest, DUI page from receipt of the order, which is normally served at arrest). CA-112 to CA-117 correct.
- **DUI fine and jail**: VC 23536 (96 hours, 48 continuous, to 6 months; $390 to $1,000). 10 years on record (HB Section 9). Open container: HB Section 9 (trunk, not glove box; bus, taxi, camper, motorhome exempt).
- **Turn on red and red arrows**: HB Sections 6 and 7 and VC 21453: right on red after stop unless NO TURN ON RED; left on red one-way to one-way; red arrow means no turn until green. CA-051 to CA-055 correct. Core ch03 line 63 defers to the box; no conflict.

## Core text vs California law

No core sentence around the 23 markers contradicts California law. Notes:
- ch13 line 53 promised a rider rule the insert lacked: fixed in the insert.
- ch09 Section 9.3 (Motorcycles) says nothing about **lane splitting**, which is legal in California (VC 21658.1; HB Section 7 mentions it). "Never share a lane with a motorcycle" is still correct, but a CA reader may meet a rider passing between lanes. There is no insert key for it. Owner decision: add a `motorcycles` insert key or leave as is.
- ch12 line 106 (belt fit checks) becomes California law on 2027-01-01 (AB 435); the insert now points to it.
