## Law changes checked (2025 and 2026)

- **Hands-free law (Siddens Bening, RSMo 304.822):** in force since Aug. 28, 2023; tickets since Jan. 1, 2025. Covered in phone_laws. A 2025 House bill (2858H) would change it; no evidence it passed.
- **New laws effective Aug. 28, 2026 (NEW26):** school bus passing 5 points and class D felony if serious injury or death; MoDOT may raise rural interstate limits to 75 mph after studies; DWI causing death by criminal negligence now a class A felony. Covered where testable (school_bus, speed_limits).
- **Vehicle safety inspections:** SB 1408 (2026) would end most passenger vehicle safety inspections; passage and effective date not confirmed (carscoops.com, May 2026; FOX4, May 2026). Left out of the book.
