## Recent and pending changes checked
- 50-hour practice law (P.L.2023, c.216) in force for permits issued on or after Feb. 1, 2025; MVC began accepting BA-CSD on Aug. 1, 2025.
- Insurance minimums rose to $35,000 / $70,000 on Jan. 1, 2026 (N.J.S.A. 39:6A-3 schedule).
- Pending, not law: S54 (2026 session, unlicensed driving penalties); A1076 (2026, knowledge test in Punjabi). A law adding pedestrian and cyclist safety to driver education was reported in the press; it changes curriculum, not the MVC test (article could not be loaded).

## Unverified or left out
- Turning on a **steady red arrow**: N.J.S.A. 39:4-115 does not mention arrows; the insert states the MUTCD default (no turn on a red arrow). Confirm against the current manual.
- **Following distance** taught by the manual: the insert uses the Division of Highway Traffic Safety figure (2 seconds; 4 or 5 in poor conditions). Confirm against the manual.
- Low-beam distance when **following** a vehicle: 39:3-60 gives no figure; the insert gives none.
- Probationary license fee, knowledge and road test fees (not on the MVC fee page).
- Child restraint fine (statute page failed to load).
- Daytime running lights rule and any wiper-rule fine.
- HOV: no general carpool lanes identified in New Jersey; insert set to OMIT.
- Police stop steps from the manual (insert uses Attorney General Safe Stop tips and N.J.S.A. 39:3-29).
