# New Jersey inserts

## school_zone

In a {{STATE}} school zone, the speed limit is **25 mph**. It applies during recess, when children can be seen from the road, and while children are going to or leaving school at the start and end of the day. At other times, the normal posted limit applies.

## work_zone

In {{STATE}}, fines **double** for speeding and many other moving violations in a construction or repair zone marked by signs or traffic control devices. Workers do not have to be present. A missing or stolen sign is no excuse in court. The same doubled fines apply on roads posted as **safe corridors**.

## turn_on_red

In {{STATE}}, you may turn **right on a red light** unless a sign forbids it. Stop completely first, look in every direction, and yield to traffic and to pedestrians in the crosswalk you are turning across.

**Left on red is never allowed** in {{STATE}}, not even from a one-way street onto another one-way street.

A **steady red arrow** is a stop signal too: after a full stop you may turn right on it unless a sign forbids it. Never turn left on one.

## move_over

{{STATE}}'s Move Over law protects stopped vehicles with flashing lights: police, fire and ambulance (red or blue), tow trucks, highway maintenance and sanitation trucks (amber or yellow). It also covers any **disabled vehicle** showing hazard lights, flares or reflective triangles.

Change into a lane that is **not next to** the stopped vehicle. If you cannot, slow to a safe speed **below the posted limit** and be ready to stop.

Fine: **$100 to $500**.

## emergency_vehicles

In {{STATE}}, when an emergency vehicle comes with its siren sounding, pull over to the **right-hand edge** or curb, clear of any intersection, and stop until it passes. State law gives no different edge for one-way streets. Obey a police officer who directs you otherwise.

Never follow a responding emergency vehicle closer than **300 feet**. Do not drive or park within **200 feet** of fire trucks stopped at a fire.

## school_bus

When a {{STATE}} school bus flashes its **red lights**, stop at least **25 feet** away, from either direction, until the lights go off.

On a **divided highway** with a safety island or a barrier between the roadways, traffic on the other side does not stop. It must slow to **10 mph** or less until past the bus and the children.

Passing a stopped school bus: a fine of at least **$100**, up to 15 days in jail or 15 days of community service, and **5 points**.

## u_turn

In {{STATE}}, never make a U-turn on a curve, on the approach to or near the top of a hill, or anywhere your car cannot be seen from **500 feet** away in both directions. Never make one where a NO U TURN sign is posted. Everywhere else, turn only when it is safe and you do not block traffic.

## speed_limits

{{STATE}} limits when no sign says otherwise:

| Where | Limit |
|---|---|
| School zone (recess, children present) | 25 mph |
| Business or residential district | 25 mph |
| Suburban business or residential district | 35 mph |
| Other roads | 50 mph |
| Posted highways | 55 or 65 mph |

| Over the limit by | Points |
|---|---|
| 1 to 14 mph | 2 |
| 15 to 29 mph | 4 |
| 30 mph or more | 5 |

## following_distance

{{STATE}}'s Division of Highway Traffic Safety teaches at least **2 seconds** behind the car ahead in good weather. On wet or slippery roads or in poor weather, stretch it to **4 or 5 seconds**.

Following too closely (tailgating) costs **5 points** and a fine of **$140** or more.

## parking_rules

In {{STATE}}, never park within:

| Place | Feet |
|---|---|
| Fire hydrant | 10 |
| Crosswalk or intersection | 25 |
| Stop sign | 50 |
| Nearest rail of a railroad crossing | 50 |
| Fire station driveway (same side) | 20 |

Where signs are posted, the fire station limit is **75 feet** on the opposite side of the street.

Park parallel to the curb, **within 6 inches** of it, facing the way traffic moves.

## bicycle_passing

When you approach a person on a bicycle, on foot or on a scooter, {{STATE}} law gives you three steps:

1. If you can, **change lanes** to one that is not next to them.
2. If you cannot, leave at least **4 feet** while passing.
3. If neither is possible, slow to **25 mph** or less and be ready to stop.

Fine: **$100**. If someone is injured: **$500** and **2 points**.

## crash_reporting

In {{STATE}}, report a crash to the police **right away** if anyone is injured or killed, or if damage to anyone's property is over **$500**.

If no police officer files a report, you must send a written report to the MVC within **10 days**. Knowingly skipping a report costs **$30 to $100**, and the MVC may suspend your license.

## bac_limits

{{STATE}} sets three limits:

| Driver | Illegal at a BAC of |
|---|---|
| Age 21 or older | 0.08 percent |
| Commercial vehicle driver | 0.04 percent |
| Under 21 | 0.01 percent |

Even below 0.08, other evidence that alcohol has impaired you can support a drunk driving charge.

## implied_consent

By driving in {{STATE}}, you agree to a breath test when police arrest you for drunk driving.

A **first refusal** costs a fine of **$300 to $500**, a class at an Intoxicated Driver Resource Center, and your license until an **ignition interlock** is installed. The interlock then stays for **9 to 15 months** after your license comes back.

The MVC also charges a refusal surcharge of **$1,000 a year for three years**.

## dui_penalties

First drunk driving offense in {{STATE}}:

| BAC | Fine | Interlock |
|---|---|---|
| 0.08 to 0.099 | $250 to $400 | 3 months |
| 0.10 to 0.149 | $300 to $500 | 7 to 12 months |
| 0.15 or more | $300 to $500 | 12 to 15 months, after 3 months without a license |

No driving until the interlock is in. Add up to **30 days** in jail, **12 to 48 hours** at an Intoxicated Driver Resource Center, and a **$1,000** surcharge each year for three years.

**Open containers** are banned for everyone in the car: **$200** the first time.

## underage_alcohol

A {{STATE}} driver **under 21** with a BAC of **0.01** or more loses the license for **30 to 90 days**, performs **15 to 30 days** of community service, and must complete an alcohol education program. If you have no license yet, the suspension starts when you become eligible.

Off the road, underage drinking or possession brings written warnings for the first offenses. Buying alcohol, or lying about your age to get it, costs at least **$500**.

## phone_laws

In {{STATE}}, every driver must keep phones and other devices **hands free**. Holding one to talk, text or browse is illegal, except to report a fire, crash, road hazard or emergency.

| Offense | Fine |
|---|---|
| First | $200 to $400 |
| Second | $400 to $600 |
| Third or later | $600 to $800, plus 3 points and a possible 90-day suspension |

Permit and probationary drivers may not use any device at all, **even hands free**.

## seat_belts

In {{STATE}}, the driver and every passenger in **every seat** must wear a seat belt. The driver is responsible for passengers aged **8 to 17**.

The belt fine is **$20**, with no points. Under the GDL rules, a permit or probationary driver whose passenger is unbelted also breaks a license condition, which costs **$100**.

## child_restraints

{{STATE}} children ride in the **back seat** when the car has one:

| Child | Required seat |
|---|---|
| Under 2 and under 30 pounds | Rear-facing seat with a five-point harness |
| Under 4 and under 40 pounds | Rear-facing until outgrown, then forward-facing with a harness |
| Under 8 and under 57 inches | Harness seat until outgrown, then a booster seat |

Never put a rear-facing seat in front of an active passenger airbag.

## vehicle_rules

In {{STATE}}, a passenger car is inspected **every two years**. A new car gets its first inspection after **five years**. You may inspect up to two months before the due date shown on the windshield sticker.

Tell the MVC about a new address within **one week**. The fine is up to $25.

Carry your license, registration and insurance card every time you drive.

## police_stop

{{STATE}}'s Attorney General gives these tips. Lower your window, and at night turn on the inside light. Turn down the music. Keep your hands on the wheel and avoid sudden moves. Tell the officer before you reach for your documents.

After you hand over your license, registration and insurance card, you do not have to answer other questions. If you think the stop was unfair, comply now and file a complaint later.

Failing to show your documents costs **$150**.

## headlight_use

In {{STATE}}, your headlights must be on:

- from a **half hour after sunset** to a **half hour before sunrise**;
- whenever smoke, fog or poor light keeps you from seeing people and vehicles **500 feet** ahead;
- whenever rain, mist or snow makes you use your **wipers**.

## driver_record

{{STATE}} counts points. At **12 points**, your license is suspended. Six or more points within three years brings a yearly surcharge: **$150**, plus $25 for each point above six, for three years.

You lose **3 points** for every full year with no violation or suspension. A state approved defensive driving course removes **2 points**, once every five years.

Minimum standard insurance: **$35,000** per person and **$70,000** per crash for injuries, **$25,000** for property damage, and **$15,000** in personal injury protection.

## road_test

The {{STATE}} road test lasts about **10 to 15 minutes**, on an off-road course or a public road. The Safety Specialist scores reverse driving, stops, corners, posture, steering, turning around, **parallel parking** and yielding.

Bring a {{STATE}} licensed driver at least **21** with three years of driving experience; only the examiner rides along. Under 21, both **GDL decals** must be on the car. Self-parking cars and tinted windows are turned away; backup cameras are allowed. Pass, and the examiner endorses your permit; fail, and you wait **14 days**.

## supervised_hours

{{STATE}} drivers whose permit was issued **under 21** must log supervised practice before the license:

| Category | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Total hours | At least 50 |
| In darkness | At least 10 |
| Supervised period | At least 6 months |

A parent, guardian or supervising driver certifies the hours on **form BA-CSD**. Your supervisor sits in the front seat and must be a {{STATE}} licensed driver at least **21** with at least **three years** of experience.

## signal_distance

In {{STATE}}, signal your turn continuously for at least the last **100 feet** before you turn.

## headlight_dimming

In {{STATE}}, switch to low beams when an oncoming vehicle comes within **500 feet**. Also dim your lights when you follow close behind another vehicle, so your high beams do not glare in its mirrors.
