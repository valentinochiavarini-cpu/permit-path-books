# Chapter 1: Your Path to a {{STATE}} License

A {{STATE}} license comes in clear stages, and nearly every delay comes from a missing paper or a skipped step. The {{STATE}} Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC) runs licensing. Fees and forms change, so check its website before every visit.

> **On the test**
>
> - The two permits: the special learner permit at 16 and the examination permit at 17.
> - The 50 practice hours, 10 of them in darkness, and the six-month supervised period.
> - Probationary license limits: one passenger, no driving from 11:01 p.m. to 5 a.m., no phones of any kind.
> - The red decals on both license plates for drivers under 21.

## 1.1 The Stages of the Graduated License

{{STATE}} uses a **Graduated Driver License (GDL)** program. You earn freedom in steps, because new drivers crash most in their first months alone. In {{STATE}}, the GDL rules cover every new driver **under 21**, not only teens.

| Stage | Earliest age | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| Special learner permit | 16 | Driving school lessons, then supervised practice |
| Examination permit | 17 | Supervised practice only |
| Probationary license | 17 | Driving alone, with limits |
| Basic license | 18 | Full privileges |

There are two ways in. At **16**, you enroll in a behind-the-wheel course at your high school or a licensed driving school and get a **special learner permit**. At **17**, you can skip the course and buy an **examination permit** at an MVC driver testing center. Either way, nobody gets a license before 17.

Cars, pickups and vans need a **Class D** (basic auto) license.

## 1.2 Paperwork and What to Bring

Every applicant fills out **form BA-208**, the permit and license application, and proves identity with the **6 Points of ID** system: each document is worth a set number of points, and yours must add up to at least **6**. The MVC website's document selector shows which mix works.

Bring:

- Identity documents worth 6 points, as paper originals.
- **One proof of your {{STATE}} address** for a standard license, such as a bank statement, a utility bill or a lease.
- Your **Social Security number**. If you have none, an ITIN works, and if you have neither, you may sign an affidavit.
- A **certified English translation** of any document in another language.
- Glasses or contacts if you wear them.

**Under 18.** A parent or guardian signs a consent form. The same form can also certify your home address if no bill comes in your name. For the special learner permit, the driving school helps you complete **form BA-412D**.

**REAL ID.** In {{STATE}}, REAL ID is **optional**. A standard license still lets you drive, vote and prove who you are. It is no longer accepted to board a domestic flight, so you would need a passport or another accepted ID instead. A REAL ID costs **$11** more and needs **two** proofs of address instead of one.

> **Road tip:** Lay out every document the night before and check that your name is spelled the same on each. A mismatch gets you sent home.

## 1.4 The Knowledge Test

You take the knowledge test with the vision test at an MVC driver testing center, by appointment. On the special learner path, you take it at your driving school or high school.

| Feature | The {{STATE}} rule |
|---|---|
| Questions | 50, multiple choice |
| To pass | 80 percent: 40 correct (10 misses allowed) |
| Topics | Signs, traffic laws and safe driving, all from the {{STATE}} Driver Manual |
| Languages | Written in 13 languages; read aloud in English or Spanish |
| If you fail | Wait **7 days**, then retest |

If your language is not offered, ask for a state interpreter when you book; those appointments can take four to six weeks.

> **Test trap:** Fifty questions sounds like plenty of room, but eleven misses is a fail. Study the permit limits, practice hours and alcohol rules, not only signs.

When you pass, your permit is **validated** for practice driving. The permit is good for **two years**. If you do not finish the knowledge test, practice period and road test in that time, you buy a new permit and start again.

## 1.5 The Vision Test

The MVC checks your eyes at the testing center before the knowledge test. You need at least **20/50** in each eye, with or without glasses or contacts. If you have sight in only one eye, that eye must reach 20/50.

Pass only with glasses or contacts, and your license requires you to wear them to drive. If you fail, your doctor fills out **form ST-14** for you to bring back.

## 1.6 Driving on a Permit

### The Special Learner Path

Your first driving is **6 hours** of lessons with a licensed instructor in a dual-control car. Then the permit is validated at an MVC agency, and supervised practice with your family can begin.

### Rules for Every Permit Driver Under 21

**Your supervisor.** A {{STATE}} licensed driver at least **21** who has held a license for at least **three years**. The supervisor sits in the **front seat** beside you.

**Passengers.** Besides your supervisor, you may carry your parent or guardian and your own dependents (your child, not a brother or sister). Beyond them, only **one** more passenger, unless a parent or guardian is in the car.

**Hours.** No driving from **11:01 p.m. to 5 a.m.**

**Devices.** No cell phone, handheld video game or other wireless device, **not even hands free**. You may use a phone only to report an emergency.

**Seat belts.** You and every passenger must wear one.

**Decals.** Show a red, reflective **GDL decal** on the upper left corner of **both** the front and rear license plates of any car you drive.

Breaking any of these rules costs a **$100** fine.

### The Practice Requirement

Before the road test, a driver whose permit was issued under 21 must:

1. Practice under supervision for at least **six months** after the permit is validated.
2. Log at least **50 hours** of practice, including at least **10 hours in darkness**.

Your parent, guardian or supervising driver certifies the hours on **form BA-CSD**, the Certification of Supervised Driving, and you hand it in when you get your license. A false certification costs you your driving privileges for **six months**.

> **Remember it:** Six months, 50 hours, 10 in the dark. Both conditions apply: finishing the hours early does not shorten the wait. Use the driving log at the back of this book.

## 1.7 The Probationary License

After you pass the road test, you hold a **probationary license** for at least one year: your first license to drive alone. Under 21, these limits apply:

**Hours.** No driving from **11:01 p.m. to 5 a.m.** The only exceptions are for work or religious activities, and you must carry a signed letter on the employer's or religious group's letterhead that explains why.

**Passengers.** Parents, guardians and your dependents, plus **one** other passenger. With a parent or guardian along, the limit does not apply.

**Devices.** No phone or other interactive wireless device, handheld **or** hands free, except to report an emergency. This ban covers every probationary driver, whatever the age.

**Seat belts and decals.** Everyone buckles up, and both decals stay on the plates.

**Points.** If you rack up two or more moving violations totaling four or more points while you are new, the MVC sends you to a four-hour **Probationary Driver Program** class. Skip it, or get another violation within a year after it, and your license is suspended.

### Moving Up to a Basic License

After **one year** of driving alone on a probationary license, and once you are at least **18**, you can upgrade to a basic license online. If you never upgrade, you must make an appointment to renew the probationary license within three and a half years.

> **Test trap:** The limit starts at 11:01 p.m., not midnight. A friend who is 21 does not lift the passenger limit; only a parent or guardian does. And "hands free" is still banned for permit and probationary drivers.

## 1.8 The Road Test

### Scheduling and What to Bring

Book your road test online through the MVC portal or at a driver testing center. The earliest date is **six months** after your permit was validated (three months if you are 21 or older).

Bring:

- The same 6 Points of ID you used for your permit.
- Your valid permit.
- A licensed {{STATE}} driver, at least 21 and licensed for at least three years, to drive you there.
- A registered, insured car with a current inspection sticker and its insurance card.
- **GDL decals** on both plates if you are under 21.

The examiner, a Safety Specialist, must be able to reach the parking brake or foot brake from the passenger seat. Only the examiner rides with you.

### When the Car Is Turned Away

There is no test if the car is unsafe, the inspection sticker is missing or expired, the decals are missing, the windows are tinted, the car parks itself, or worn tires, bad brakes or missing seat belts make it unsafe. A rental car works only if the contract lists you as a driver. Backup cameras and parking sensors are allowed.

### What the Examiner Scores

The test takes about **10 to 15 minutes** on an off-road course or a public road. The examiner watches:

- Driving in reverse.
- Stopping for signs, and stopping smoothly.
- How you approach corners and intersections.
- Sitting properly and steering properly.
- Turning the car around.
- **Parallel parking**.
- Yielding the right-of-way.

### After the Test

Pass, and the examiner endorses your permit. Take it to the road test site, if it issues licenses, or to any licensing center for your probationary license, with no appointment. Fail, and you wait at least **14 days** to retest.

## 1.9 Adults Getting a First License

The age that matters in {{STATE}} is **21**. Drivers aged 18 to 20 follow every GDL rule: the 50 hours, the six months, the decals, the night limit and the passenger limit.

At **21 or older**, you still buy an examination permit and pass the vision, knowledge and road tests, but:

- You practice under supervision for **three months** instead of six.
- The 50 hour certificate is not required, as long as your permit was issued at 21 or older.
- No decals, no night limit and no passenger limit.
- You still get a probationary license first, and its device ban applies to you.

A permit issued before 21 still needs the 50 hour certificate.

## 1.10 New Residents and Foreign License Holders

**Moving here from another state.** Transfer your license within **60 days** of moving, or before it expires if that is sooner. With a valid, non-probationary license in good standing from another state, D.C. or a U.S. territory, the knowledge and road tests are waived. If your license expired more than one year ago, you start over as a first-time applicant.

**Licenses from other countries.** A visitor may drive for up to one year on a foreign license carried with an International Driving Permit. To get a {{STATE}} license:

- **Taiwan or South Korea.** Under reciprocity agreements, applicants 18 or older can exchange a valid license without the knowledge or road test, with extra documents from their country.
- **Every other country.** You buy a permit and pass the vision, knowledge and road tests. Applicants outside the GDL program get a 90-day permit and need at least **20 days** of supervised practice before the road test; if you cannot show past driving experience, you follow the full GDL steps.

**Immigration status.** {{STATE}} issues the **standard** license to residents who meet the requirements, regardless of immigration status. You prove identity with 6 Points of ID and your address as usual; without a Social Security number or ITIN, you sign an affidavit. The steps, tests and GDL rules are the same as for everyone else. A REAL ID requires proof of lawful status.

## 1.11 Where to Find the Official Rules

The **{{STATE}} Driver Manual**, the source of the test questions, is free at nj.gov/mvc in English and several other languages, along with the **Share the Keys** guide for teens and parents. If this book and the manual ever disagree, the manual and current law win.

## Quick Review

- Special learner permit at 16 through a driving school (6 hours with an instructor), or an examination permit at 17.
- The knowledge test has 50 questions; you need 40 correct (80 percent). After a fail, wait 7 days.
- Under 21: six months of supervised practice and 50 hours (10 in darkness), certified on form BA-CSD.
- Permit and probationary drivers under 21: no driving 11:01 p.m. to 5 a.m., one passenger beyond family, decals on both plates.
- No phone use at all, even hands free, for permit and probationary drivers; breaking a GDL rule costs $100.
- Road test retest wait: 14 days. Basic license after one year on probation and at 18 or older.
