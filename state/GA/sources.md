# Georgia sources

All checked 2026-10-28. "Manual" is the Georgia Department of Driver Services (DDS) Drivers' Manual, 2023-2024 edition, read from the Drive copy (file ID 14L_7A1dfE4Fw3gUvaUQSddn3dvobieoo). The Drive text is OCR with errors and ends at manual page 50 (Crashes); nothing after that was needed. DDS now publishes the manual as web pages (dds.georgia.gov/drivers-manual); the testing and licensing pages were checked against the online version and matched on every Chapter 1 point. Statutes were read on law.justia.com (2025 Code of Georgia), and the 2026 bill HB 1161 on gov.georgia.gov. Page numbers are printed manual page numbers.

## Law changes checked
- **HB 1161 (signed, effective July 1, 2026):** adds law enforcement vehicles to the yield rule (40-6-74(a)); new traffic stop rule letting drivers continue to a safe place (40-6-74(b), 40-8-91); fleeing penalties; window tint front side limit 28 percent; school zone camera rules (sign rule July 1, 2027). Move Over (40-6-16) unchanged. Reflected in emergency_vehicles and police_stop.
- **HB 651 (2026):** school zone speed camera changes; reflected only as "cameras mail a civil ticket"; no amounts printed.
- **Move Over (Spencer Pass Law), amended July 1, 2024:** current text used.
- **Addy's Law (2024):** school bus passing fine at least $1,000; reflected.
- **Speed limits (2024 amendment to 40-6-181):** urban interstate 65 tier removed; 70 where posted on interstates. The 2023-2024 manual still lists 65 for urban interstates; the book follows the statute.
- **Hands-free law:** no change found since 2018; fines and points unchanged.
- **Super Speeder:** $200 fee unchanged in the 2025 Code.
