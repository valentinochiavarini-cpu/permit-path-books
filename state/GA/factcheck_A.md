## Changes made

### Chapter (`state/GA/ch01.md`)
1. 1.7 Losing Your License (legal error)
   - Old: "or any **4-point** offense, such as passing a stopped school bus or going **24 mph or more** over the limit."
   - New: "or any offense worth **4 or more points**, such as passing a stopped school bus or going **24 mph or more** over the limit."
   - Reason: the statute covers "any offense for which four or more points are assessable" (O.C.G.A. 40-5-57.1(a)). Passing a school bus is 6 points and 34+ mph over is 6 points (40-5-57(c)(1)(A)), so "4-point offense" was wrong for the example given.
2. Quick Review: "a single 4-point offense" changed to "a single offense worth 4 or more points" (same reason).
3. 1.1: "At 18, a clean Class D becomes a full Class C." changed to "can be traded for a full Class C." Reason: the upgrade is not automatic; the holder applies (SEC2; 40-5-24(b)(3)); 1.7 already says so.

Chapter now 2,293 words (wc), no em or en dashes, no years.
