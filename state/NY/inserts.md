# New York inserts

## school_zone

{{STATE}} school speed limits are set locally and posted. They apply on school days at the times on the sign, which fall between 7 a.m. and 6 p.m., or whenever the school zone beacons flash. A **U-turn is never allowed in a school zone**. Obey every school crossing guard as you would a traffic officer.

## work_zone

In a {{STATE}} work zone, the minimum speeding fines are **doubled**, even when no workers are present. Speeding in a work zone adds **8 points** to your record. A work zone speed limit may be posted as low as **25 mph**; with no posted limit, you must still slow down. Always obey flag persons. The Move Over law also covers road crews' vehicles showing amber lights.

## turn_on_red

In {{STATE}}, after a full stop and yielding to pedestrians and cross traffic, you may turn **right on a steady red** unless a sign, signal or marking forbids it. You may turn **left on red** only from a one-way street onto another one-way street. **No turn is allowed on a red arrow.**

In **New York City**, turning on red is illegal everywhere unless a sign allows it.

## move_over

On every {{STATE}} road, take care and slow down for a stopped police, fire or ambulance vehicle with its emergency lights on, a hazard vehicle (such as a tow truck or snowplow) with amber lights, and a volunteer's car with a blue or green light.

On a **parkway, interstate or other controlled-access highway** with more than one lane, also **move out of the lane next to it** when you safely can. There, the rule covers **any stopped vehicle**, lights or not. A violation is a moving violation.

## school_bus

In {{STATE}}, when a school bus flashes its **red lights**, traffic from **both directions** must stop before reaching it, on every road, **even across a divided highway**, and in school parking lots. The manual says stop **at least 20 feet** away. Wait until the bus moves or its driver or a police officer waves you on.

Passing a stopped bus: **8 points**; fine **$250 to $400** the first time, up to **$1,500** for a third within three years, which also revokes your license for at least six months.

## u_turn

In {{STATE}}, start a U-turn from the **left part of the lane nearest the center line**, never from the right lane. You may not make one:

- where drivers cannot see you from **500 feet** away in either direction, such as near a hilltop or curve;
- in a **business district of New York City**;
- on a **limited access expressway**, even at a median crossover;
- in a **school zone** or where NO U-TURN signs are posted.

A green left arrow allows a U-turn unless a sign forbids it.

## speed_limits

Unless a sign says otherwise:

| Where | Limit |
|---|---|
| Anywhere in {{STATE}} with no posted limit | 55 mph |
| New York City | 25 mph |
| Some rural expressways (posted) | Up to 65 mph |

Points by mph over the limit: 1 to 10, **3**; 11 to 20, **4**; 21 to 30, **6**; 31 to 40, **8**; over 40, **11**. Eleven points within 24 months can suspend your license.

## following_distance

The {{STATE}} manual teaches at least a **two-second** following distance: count from when the car ahead passes a fixed object until you reach it. Increase it to **three or four seconds** in bad weather and behind large trucks. In work zones, keep two seconds or more of braking room. Following too closely is worth **4 points**.

## hov

{{STATE}} marks lanes reserved for buses, carpools (HOV), bicycles or other special vehicles with a white **diamond** symbol. Stay out unless your vehicle meets the occupancy or other rule the signs give for the hours shown. On some city streets the solid line beside the diamond lane is broken: there, any driver may enter the lane to make a right turn at the next intersection.

## parking_rules

{{STATE}} bans parking within:

| Place | Feet |
|---|---|
| Fire hydrant (unless a licensed driver stays in the car) | 15 |
| Crosswalk at an intersection | 20 |
| Fire station driveway (75 across the street) | 20 |
| Traffic light, STOP or YIELD sign | 30 |
| Pedestrian safety area | 30 |
| Railroad crossing | 50 |

Never double park or block a lowered curb. After parallel parking, your wheels must be within **one foot** of the curb.

## bicycle_passing

{{STATE}} law sets no fixed number of feet: pass a bicycle on its **left at a safe distance** and stay over until you are well clear. Slow down as you pass, because the rush of air can push a rider off balance. Do not pass a bicycle inside a roundabout. Never open your door into a cyclist's path. Riders and passengers aged 1 to 13 must wear a helmet.

## crash_reporting

After a {{STATE}} crash, stop and show your license and insurance card, and give your name, address and insurance details. If someone is hurt, give this to police as well; if no officer is there, or a damaged car's owner is absent, report to the nearest police station as soon as you can.

File a **Report of Motor Vehicle Crash (MV-104)** with DMV within **10 days** if anyone was hurt or killed, or if any one person's property damage is **$1,000 or more**. Not reporting is a misdemeanor.

## bac_limits

{{STATE}} levels:

| Offense | BAC |
|---|---|
| Driving while ability impaired (DWAI) | More than 0.05 |
| Driving while intoxicated (DWI) | 0.08 or more |
| Aggravated DWI | 0.18 or more |
| Zero tolerance, under 21 | 0.02 to 0.07 |

Under 21 at 0.02 to 0.07: license suspended **six months**, plus a **$125** civil penalty and **$100** fee to get it back.

## implied_consent

By driving in {{STATE}} you consent to a chemical test. Refuse after an arrest and your license is **suspended at your court arraignment**. If a DMV hearing confirms the refusal, it is **revoked**, even if you are found not guilty:

- First refusal: at least **one year**, plus a **$500** civil penalty.
- Within five years of an earlier refusal or alcohol or drug offense: at least **18 months**, plus **$750**.
- Under 21, second refusal: one year or until 21, whichever is longer.

## dui_penalties

{{STATE}} first offense:

| Offense | Fine | License |
|---|---|---|
| DWAI | $300 to $500, up to 15 days | 90-day suspension |
| DWI | $500 to $1,000, up to 1 year | Revoked 6+ months |
| Aggravated DWI | $1,000 to $2,500, up to 1 year | Revoked 1+ year |

Under 21, any alcohol or drug driving conviction revokes your license for at least one year.

DWI adds an **ignition interlock**, normally 12 months; a child under 16 aboard makes it a felony.

**Open container:** no one in the car may drink alcohol, hold an open container or use cannabis.

## phone_laws

{{STATE}} bans talking on a **hand-held phone** and using any **portable electronic device** to text, email or browse while driving. Hands-free calling is allowed, as are emergency calls. Each violation: **5 points**. Fines: up to **$200** for a first offense, **$250** for a second within 18 months, **$450** for a third. Junior and probationary drivers: **120-day suspension** for a first conviction.

## seat_belts

{{STATE}} is a **primary enforcement** state: police may stop you for an unbuckled occupant alone. The **driver** and every passenger **16 or older**, in any seat, must wear a belt, one person per belt, or face a fine of up to **$50** each. The driver must also make sure every passenger **under 16** is belted or in a child seat: **$25 to $100** and **3 points** per violation.

## child_restraints

{{STATE}} requires:

| Age | Required |
|---|---|
| Under 4 | Federally approved child safety seat |
| Under 2 | Rear-facing, unless the seat's limits are outgrown |
| 4 to 7 | Booster or other child seat, with lap and shoulder belt |
| 8 to 15 | Seat belt |

A child under 8 who is over 4 feet 9 inches or 100 pounds may use the belt. Never put a rear-facing seat in front of an active airbag.

## vehicle_rules

{{STATE}} liability insurance minimums: **$25,000/$50,000** for injury, **$50,000/$100,000** for death, **$10,000** property damage. Turn in your plates before coverage ends; 90 uninsured days suspend your license. Vehicles need an inspection every year. Use headlights from a half hour after sunset to a half hour before sunrise, when you can see less than **1,000 feet**, and whenever wipers are on. Report an address change within **10 days**; new residents register within **30 days**.

## road_test

The {{STATE}} examiner scores leaving the curb, turns and intersections, parallel parking, a **three-point turn**, driving in traffic and vehicle control. Parking assist and cruise control may not be used. Bring your permit, course certificate, and under 18 the signed **MV-262**. Only your accompanying driver may ride along. Results post online after 6 p.m.; after a fail, wait **14 days** to retest. Failing your first two tests means paying for more.

## supervised_hours

{{STATE}} drivers under 18 must complete before the road test:

| Category | Hours |
|---|---|
| Total | 50 |
| After sunset | At least 15 |
| Moderate to heavy traffic | 10 recommended |

Night hours count toward the 50, and so do driver education hours. A parent or guardian signs the **Certification of Supervised Driving (MV-262)**. Every supervisor must be 21 or older and licensed for the vehicle; regional limits on who supervises appear in Chapter 1.

## signal_distance

{{STATE}} law requires your turn signal or hand signal at least **100 feet** before you turn or change lanes, and at least 100 feet before an expressway exit ramp. When you can, signal before you start to brake.

## headlight_dimming

In {{STATE}}, use low beams within **500 feet** of an oncoming vehicle and within **200 feet** of a vehicle you are following, even if it is in another lane.
