## Differences and open points

1. **Supervised hours.** The run brief mentioned 15 hours in moderate to heavy traffic. The current MV-262 (3/25) requires 50 hours with 15 after sunset and says nothing about traffic; the manual only recommends 10 hours in moderate to heavy traffic. The book prints 10 as recommended.
2. **Online permit test.** PERMIT says the online option is for applicants under 18; ONLINE describes an MV-264 attestation for applicants 18 and older. The book says only that minors test online with a parent or guardian supervising, without stating that adults are excluded or included. The online pass standard (80 percent, half the sign questions) differs from the office test (14 of 20, 2 of 4 signs); the number of online questions is not published.
3. **Exchange window.** The manual says a license expired more than one year needs testing; XCH (newer) says valid or expired less than 24 months. The book follows XCH. XCH is inconsistent about Canada in one sentence; both MAN and DINY include Canadian provinces.
4. **Points.** New point values took effect 2026-02-16 (school bus 8, work zone speeding 8, alcohol or drug 11, look-back 24 months). The manual still shows the older table.
5. **NYC school speed limits.** NYC plans 15 mph in school slow zones under Sammy's Law (news report, March 2026; not yet in effect, not a DMV source). Left out.
6. **Not verified, left out:** an office written test retest wait or attempt limit; the separate road test fee amount; a list of automatic road test fails; the fee for REAL ID versus standard (no surcharge found on FEES); foreign license exchange agreements (DMV lists none; only bills were found). Pending bills (for example S4250, a learner sign rule) are not law.
