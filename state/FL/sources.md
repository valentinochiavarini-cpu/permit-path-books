# Florida sources

All checked 2026-10-28. "HB" is the Official Florida Driver License Handbook, rev. 08/2023 (footer of every page), read from the Drive copy (file ID 1bDRChO0h4R1B1OfBvwqpp-wG5eRvamPX). The Drive text stops at handbook page 72 (Night Driving), so pages 72 to 99 (night driving, crashes, sharing the road, school buses) were filled from statutes and FLHSMV pages. Page numbers are handbook page numbers.

Newer edition: not confirmed. The FLHSMV handbook PDF (flhsmv.gov/pdf/handbooks/englishdriverhandbook.pdf) could not be opened here (proxy 403, WebFetch client error), and the handbooks page lists no revision date. The rev. 08/2023 text is out of date on three points used in this book, each taken from the newer primary source instead: (1) teens now need a driver education course, not TLSAE, for a learner's license (2026 Statutes); (2) exams are English only; (3) the police crash report threshold is now $2,000.
