# North Carolina sources

All checked 2026-10-28. "HB" is the North Carolina Driver Handbook, revised May 2025 (back cover: "Revised May 2025"), read from the Drive copy (file ID 1xsHtFARBh7SuBf0V7-TNRynnxLqu0gRf). It is the edition on MyNCDMV.gov; no newer edition was found. The handbook predates four law changes used in this edition: the Level 3 driving log repeal (S.L. 2025-91), the Driving Eligibility Certificate repeal (S.L. 2026-46), the higher insurance minimums (G.S. 20-279.1 as amended, effective July 1, 2025) and the 25 mph unpaved road limit (S.L. 2026-46, effective December 1, 2026). Where the handbook and current law differ, the book follows current law.
