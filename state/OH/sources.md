# Ohio sources

All checked 2026-10-28. "MAN" is the **Ohio Driver Manual, HSY 7607 10/25** (BMV, edition dated October 2025, 55 pages), read in full from the Drive copy (file ID 16-Z3HYqpZnD0mmZ_MLkdbnQ3Hf06YGyr). No newer edition was found; the BMV pages checked match it on every Chapter 1 point. Page numbers are the manual's printed page numbers. The manual's speed limit table (p. 9) lost its numbers in the text export, so speed limits come from the statute.

Short names:
- **GDL**: BMV, Driver License and ID Cards, https://ohiobmv.gov/dl-gdl.aspx (knowledge test, online test, TIPIC, driver ed, BMV 5791, probationary limits, road test)
- **FEES**: BMV fee schedule, https://www.ohiobmv.gov/doc-fees.aspx (driver license fees marked "Last Updated 9/30/2025")
- **NEW**: BMV, New to Ohio, https://ohiobmv.gov/new-to-ohio.aspx
- **ORC s**: Ohio Revised Code section s, https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-s (effective date of the version read given where it matters)
- **OAC r**: Ohio Administrative Code rule r, https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-administrative-code/rule-r
- **OSU**: Ohio State Office of International Affairs, https://oia.osu.edu/international-students/life-ohio-state/drivers-license-and-id-cards (secondary, reciprocity list only, matches MAN p. 3)
- **ABC6**: https://abc6onyourside.com/news/local/smartlane-opens-wednesday-on-670-east (I-670 SmartLane; news source, used only for the app-only HOV box)

## Law changes checked (2025 and 2026)

- HB 54 (136th GA), eff. 2025-06-30: amended ORC 4511.204 (handheld device law), 4511.13 and 4511.21. Current text used.
- HB 37 and SB 100 (135th GA), eff. 2025-04-09: amended ORC 4511.19 and 4510.31; OVI reinstatement fee now $315 (FEES).
- HB 96 (136th GA), eff. 2025-09-30: amended ORC 4511.75 (school bus). Stop distance and four-lane rule unchanged.
- HB 184 (136th GA), eff. 2026-03-20: amended ORC 4507.05, 4507.21, 4507.23. Current text read; permit age, supervisors, curfew, driver ed and 50/10 rules unchanged in substance. Exact HB 184 changes not identified (bill page gave no summary).
- HB 434 (136th GA), signed 2025-11-25: exempts certain military-related limited term applicants from driver ed. Not taught (too narrow).
- Limited term TIPIC holders must meet driver training rules and bring BMV 5791, effective 2025-06-30 (GDL).
- Pending, not law as of 2026-10-28: SB 419 (passed Senate; permit at 15, one-year permit hold, permit valid 18 months); Sub. HB 463 (in House Transportation Committee; one-year permit hold, app-based practice option, new abbreviated adult course hours). Both would change Chapter 1.

## Not verified or left out

- Dimming distance in feet: Ohio statute (ORC 4513.15) and manual give none, so no figure is printed.
- E-Check (emissions) left out: under active change (state moving to end it in seven counties, 2026 news).
- Fine for adult belt is secondary enforcement; no separate fine verified for the driver's duty to belt passengers.
- Points for passing a school bus: not listed in ORC 4510.036 table read; not printed.
- Whether the 21 and older licensee must hold the TIPIC a minimum time (other than temporary residents): manual and BMV give none; not printed.
- ohiobmv.gov new residents and fee pages under bmv.ohio.gov returned errors; the ohiobmv.gov versions above were used.
