# Michigan sources

All checked 2026-10-28. "MAN" is **What Every Driver Must Know** (Michigan Department of State, cover dated October 2025), read from the Drive copy (file ID 1cHIqQJlfm6bpVbKbz3dgGNZm1Wx-4gzf). The text export stops partway through Chapter 7 (after "Do not take risks"): the sections "If your car becomes disabled", "If you are pulled over", the Safe Driving Knowledge Test and the resources pages are missing, so the police stop box uses the Michigan State Police page instead. No newer edition was found. The export has no reliable page numbers, so MAN references give chapter and section.

Short names:
- **U18**: SOS, New drivers under 18, https://www.michigan.gov/sos/license-id/new-drivers-under-18 (Segments, GDL levels, Level 1 fee, skills test prerequisites)
- **FAQ**: SOS FAQ, Drivers under 18, https://www.michigan.gov/sos/faqs/license-and-id/drivers-under-18 (Level 2 violations, authorized activities, Kelsey's Law)
- **A18**: SOS, New drivers 18 and older, https://www.michigan.gov/sos/license-id/new-drivers-18-older (knowledge test, TIP, fees, skills test rules, probation)
- **OT**: SOS online testing, https://www.michigan.gov/SOSOnlineTesting, and SOS news release https://www.michigan.gov/sos/resources/news/2025/07/02/michigan-secretary-of-state-now-offers-online-drivers-license-testing-for-adults
- **DST**: SOS-360 Driving Skills Test Study Guide (rev. 03/2021), https://www.michigan.gov/documents/ROAD_SKILLS_TEST_STUDY_GUIDE_05-02_21935_7.pdf
- **MSP-STOP**: Michigan State Police, What to expect during a traffic stop, https://www.michigan.gov/msp/services/safetytips/what-to-expect-during-a-traffic-stop
- **MSP-OWI**: Michigan State Police OHSP, Impaired driving law, https://www.michigan.gov/msp/divisions/ohsp/safety-programs/Impaired-Driving/impaired-driving-law (note: still lists Driver Responsibility fees, which are outdated; not used)
- **MCL s**: Michigan Vehicle Code or other statute, https://www.legislature.mi.gov/documents/mcl/pdf/mcl-s.pdf (PDF version read; the HTML pages block automated reading)
- **SFA**: Senate Fiscal Agency analysis of HB 4250 to 4252 (hands-free law), https://hercules-milenia.legislature.mi.gov/documents/2023-2024/billanalysis/Senate/htm/2023-SFA-4250-F.htm
- **BRIDGE**: Bridge Michigan, hands-free law explainer, https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/michigans-new-hands-free-driving-law-takes-effect-friday-what-know/ (news, used only for crash doubling and points; points confirmed in MCL 257.320a)
- **WILX**: https://www.wilx.com/2025/04/01/michigans-new-car-seat-law-takes-effect-april-2 (news, effective date of the new car seat law; rules confirmed in MCL 257.710d)

## Not verified or left out
- Number of questions and pass mark of the SOS knowledge test (Operator and Signs parts): not published by the SOS. Segment 1 and Segment 2 exam question counts: not published.
- Level 2 license fee: not listed by the SOS.
- Seat belt and child seat fine amounts, property protection insurance amount, vision acuity numbers (R 257.3 has a pending revision), studded tire rules: left out.
- Hands-free second offense: MAN and SFA say $250; Bridge Michigan said $200. Used $250.
- Move Over civil fine ($400 in MCL 257.653a): left out.
- MAN still describes the older child seat rule; the statute in force since April 2, 2025 is used.
