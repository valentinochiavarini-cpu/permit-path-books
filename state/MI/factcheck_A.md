## Open items

1. Kelsey's Law and built-in voice systems: the SOS FAQ still says the ban "does not apply if the teen is using a voice-operated system that is integrated into the vehicle". The current MCL 257.602c (am. 2023 PA 40) has no such exception, and MAN Ch. 4 says GDL 1 and 2 teens may not make or receive non-emergency calls. The chapter follows the statute ("not even hands-free"). The FAQ looks outdated; keep watching.
2. Adult license fee: NEWS says that passing the road test before the TIP expires means the license "is issued at no charge"; A18 lists the TIP fee and a $25 standard license fee without saying whether a first-time adult pays both. The fee table is accurate line by line but could suggest an adult pays $25 twice. Owner may want a note under the table once confirmed (MCL 257.811 not checked: legislature HTML/PDF fetch via curl blocked).
