## Unresolved or notes (no action needed)

- PUB 95 PDF edition date still unconfirmed (pa.gov lists no date). All Chapter 1 figures match the current online manual.
- ch01 line 121 ("five belts, five people, you included") reads 4581(a)(3) as counting the driver among belted occupants. The statute says "passengers" may not exceed "available" belts and does not define either term; the book's reading is the conservative, practical one and matches the manual's intent.
