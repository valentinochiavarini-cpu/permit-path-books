## Conflicts and open points

- **Move Over fines.** PUB 95 (4-21) and STAT 3327(b) say up to $500, $1,000 and $2,000. The online "Special Circumstances and Emergencies" page says $250, $500 and $1,000 and gives no speed figure. The book follows the statute.
- **Seat belt enforcement for under 18.** STAT 4581(b) needs another violation only for adults in front seats (a)(2)(ii), so a belt violation by a driver or passenger under 18 can be cited alone. This reading comes from the statute text; PennDOT pages do not state it directly.
- **HOV occupancy rule on I-279.** PennDOT's January 2023 release headline says the HOV 2+ rule was lifted outbound; the body text was not readable. The insert gives no occupancy figure and tells drivers to follow the entrance signs.
- **Manual edition.** Whether pa.gov now hosts a printed PUB 95 newer than 4-21 could not be confirmed (the PDF did not download). The online manual matched on every Chapter 1 figure.
- **Pending law.** HB 1093 (2025, seat belts for minors) is still at the bill stage; not in the book.
- **docs/states.md "Licenses regardless of status: unverified" for PA.** Not checked in this run (not needed for the book).
