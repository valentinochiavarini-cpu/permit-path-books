## Not verified or left out

- **Knowledge test question count.** Neither DPS (handbook, DPS pages) nor TDLR rules (37 TAC 15.52, 15.64; 16 TAC 84.50) publish it. Only third-party prep sites give counts, and those were not used. The book says DPS does not publish a count.
- **545.102 U-turn distance.** The section title (turning on a curve or crest of a grade) was confirmed; the statute text, which likely sets a visibility distance, could not be read. The insert states the rule without a number.
- **Red arrow turns.** 544.007 authorizes turns only on a steady red signal; it says nothing on red arrows. The insert treats a red arrow as no turn, as an inference.
- **Commercial driver BAC 0.04** and **0.15 BAC enhanced first DWI**: not in DL-7; not included in inserts.
- **HOV occupancy and toll rules** vary by lane and agency; only the handbook's example sign is given.
- **Driving retest waiting period**: DL-7 gives none beyond the 90 day, three attempt application rule.
- **Statutes site** (statutes.capitol.texas.gov) and some direct URLs (TDLR driver rules page, texas.public.law sections not surfaced by search) were blocked from this environment.
