# Texas inserts

## school_zone

In a {{STATE}} school zone, the speed shown on the School Zone sign applies while its **yellow lights flash** or during the hours posted on it. A sign at the zone may also announce that **using a wireless device is prohibited**: in a school crossing zone, a driver may not use a handheld phone while the reduced limit is in effect, except in an emergency or while stopped. Obey crossing guards and police directing traffic.

## work_zone

In a {{STATE}} construction or maintenance work zone, traffic **fines double** when workers are present. Driving around a barricade or ignoring a work zone warning sign is a fine of up to $200, and that fine doubles too when workers are present. Obey flaggers: stop in your lane when told to, and go only when they wave you on. Driving around a barricade placed where **water covers the road** is a Class B misdemeanor: up to $2,000 and 180 days in jail.

## turn_on_red

In {{STATE}}, after a full stop at a **steady red light**, you may turn **right**, and you may turn **left only from a one-way street onto another one-way street**. Yield first to pedestrians and to traffic with the green. A posted sign can forbid either turn.

The law that grants these turns speaks only of a steady red light and says nothing about a **red arrow**. Treat a red arrow as no turn: stop and wait for a green arrow or a green light.

## move_over

{{STATE}}'s Slow Down or Move Over law covers stopped police, fire and ambulance vehicles, tow trucks, utility vehicles, and TxDOT or other road work vehicles with lights on. You must either **leave the lane next to them** (on a road with two or more lanes your way) or **slow to 20 mph below the limit**, or to 5 mph if the limit is under 25 mph. Fine: **$500 to $1,250**; a repeat within five years, $1,000 to $2,000 and possible suspension. Causing bodily injury is a Class A misdemeanor.

## school_bus

In {{STATE}}, stop **before reaching** a school bus showing flashing red lights, from either direction. The law sets no fixed distance in feet. Wait until the bus moves, the driver signals you on, or the lights stop. You need not stop if the bus is on a **separate roadway** of a divided highway (a barrier, unpaved space or raised median between you; a center turn lane does not count). Fine: **$500 to $1,250**; a repeat within five years, $1,000 to $2,000 and up to six months' suspension.

## u_turn

{{STATE}} prohibits a U-turn wherever a NO U-TURN sign is posted. State law also bars turning around on a curve or near the top of a hill where drivers coming from either direction cannot see your car from **500 feet** away.

## speed_limits

{{STATE}} limits unless a sign says otherwise:

| Where | Limit |
|---|---|
| Alley | 15 mph |
| Public beach (where declared) | 15 mph |
| Urban district | 30 mph |
| Unnumbered road outside a city | 60 mph |
| Numbered state or U.S. highway outside a city | 70 mph |

Posted limits on some highways reach 75 to 85 mph. Speeds set for a road apply day and night alike.

## following_distance

The {{STATE}} Driver Handbook sets a minimum gap measured in seconds. At **30 mph or less**, keep at least **2 seconds** behind the vehicle ahead in good conditions; **above 30 mph**, keep **4 seconds**. Add more time on wet, crowded or dark roads, and leave **4 to 6 seconds** behind a motorcycle. A beginner should use at least four seconds at any speed.

## hov

{{STATE}}'s HOV lanes, found mainly in its largest metro areas, post their own rules at each entrance: the minimum number of riders, the hours and the direction. A typical sign reserves a lane for **buses and carpools from 6 a.m. to 9 a.m., Monday through Friday**. Some managed lanes also charge a toll. Read the signs before you enter, and enter and leave only at marked openings.

## parking_rules

{{STATE}} bans parking within:

| Place | Feet |
|---|---|
| Fire hydrant | 15 |
| Crosswalk at an intersection | 20 |
| Fire station driveway, same side | 20 |
| Fire station entrance, opposite side | 75 |
| Roadside stop sign, yield sign or signal | 30 |
| Nearest rail of a railroad crossing | 50 |

Park within **18 inches** of the curb. Disability space without plate or placard: **$500 to $750** (first offense).

## bicycle_passing

{{STATE}} law sets **no statewide minimum distance** for passing a bicycle. Give the rider a safe gap and change lanes when you can. A cyclist may use the full lane when it is too narrow to share or under 14 feet wide, or when avoiding hazards or turning left. Some cities set a distance by ordinance; San Antonio, for example, requires **3 feet** for cars and **6 feet** for large trucks.

## crash_reporting

Stop at or near the scene of a {{STATE}} crash. If no one is hurt and the cars are drivable, move them off the main lanes to a shoulder, frontage road or cross street. Call police if anyone is hurt or killed, a car cannot be moved, a driver leaves or someone seems impaired. Exchange names, addresses, insurance and registration; show your license if asked. Hit an unattended car? Find the owner or leave a note with your name and address. TxDOT keeps no driver reports; keep your own.

## bac_limits

{{STATE}} sets these limits:

| Driver | Illegal |
|---|---|
| 21 and older | 0.08 or more, or any amount that takes away normal use of mind or body |
| Under 21 | Any detectable amount of alcohol |

Under 21, driving with any detectable alcohol is the crime of Driving Under the Influence by a Minor, even below 0.08. A minor may not buy, try to buy, drink or possess alcohol.

## implied_consent

By driving in {{STATE}}, you consent to a breath or blood test if arrested for DWI. Refusing suspends your license for **180 days**, or up to **2 years** for a repeat. Testing at **0.08 or more** brings a **90 day** suspension, up to 1 year for repeats. Drivers under 21 face the same 180 days for a refusal, and a test showing any detectable alcohol suspends a minor's license for 60 days the first time.

## dui_penalties

| {{STATE}} DWI | Fine, jail | License |
|---|---|---|
| First | Up to $2,000; 72 hours to 180 days | 90 days to 1 year |
| Second | Up to $4,000; 30 days to 1 year | 180 days to 2 years |
| Third | Up to $10,000; 2 to 10 years | 180 days to 2 years |

An **open container** in the passenger area on a public road is illegal: up to $500. With a first DWI, it makes minimum jail **6 days**.

## phone_laws

{{STATE}} bans every driver from **reading, writing or sending electronic messages** on a handheld device while driving: up to $99 the first time, up to $200 after. The ban does not apply while you are stopped, and hands-free (voice) use, navigation and emergency calls are allowed. In a **school crossing zone**, no handheld phone use at all. Drivers **under 18** may not use any wireless device, even hands-free, except in an emergency. Bus drivers carrying children may not use a phone unless stopped.

## seat_belts

In {{STATE}}, the driver and **every passenger, in every seat**, must be belted. This covers pickups and SUVs. The driver is fined **$100 to $200** for each passenger under 17 not belted or in a child seat. A driver, or a passenger 15 or older, who is unbelted is fined **$25 to $50**. No one under 18 may ride in the open bed of a pickup or flatbed, with narrow exceptions such as parades and emergencies.

## child_restraints

In {{STATE}}, a child **under 8** must ride in a child passenger safety seat system used as its maker instructs, unless the child is **taller than 4 feet 9 inches**. Then the seat belt is required. The fine is **$25 to $250**. Use rear-facing and forward-facing seats and boosters as the seat labels direct until your child outgrows them.

## vehicle_rules

{{STATE}} no longer requires a yearly safety inspection for non-commercial cars; you pay a **$7.50** inspection program fee at registration instead. **Emissions testing** is still required in 18 counties around Houston, Dallas and Fort Worth, Austin, El Paso and San Antonio. Carry proof of liability insurance of at least **$30,000/$60,000/$25,000**. Register a newly bought car within **30 days**. Report an address change to DPS within **30 days**. Tires need at least **2/32 inch** of tread.

## road_test

Take the {{STATE}} road test at a DPS office or an approved school. You are graded on control, observation, lane positioning and signaling, and may have to parallel park (camera allowed, self-parking not), stop quickly from about 20 mph and back about 60 feet. You fail for breaking a law, refusing an instruction, dangerous driving or a crash, or losing **more than 30 points**. Only you and the examiner (plus an interpreter if needed) ride in the car. Bring a printed ITTD or ITAD certificate dated within **90 days**.

## supervised_hours

{{STATE}} teens complete driver education in-car work before a provisional license:

| Category | Hours |
|---|---|
| In-car observation | 7 |
| In-car instruction | 7 |
| Supervised practice, total | 30 |
| Practice at night | At least 10 (part of the 30) |

The practice supervisor is a licensed driver 21 or older with at least one year of experience, and a parent or guardian certifies the hours. You must also hold the learner license at least six months.

## signal_distance

{{STATE}} law requires you to signal continuously for at least the **last 100 feet** before you turn. Signal before you slow down or stop as well, and turn the signal off when you finish.

## headlight_dimming

In {{STATE}}, headlights must be on from **30 minutes after sunset** to **30 minutes before sunrise**, and whenever you cannot see people or vehicles clearly at **1,000 feet**. Switch to low beams within **500 feet** of an oncoming vehicle and within **300 feet** of a vehicle you are following.
