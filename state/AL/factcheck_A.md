## Verified without change
- Knowledge test: $5 each attempt, no checks; laws, signs, safe driving from MAN; automated testing; 11 foreign languages (Arabic, Chinese, Farsi, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Spanish, Thai, Vietnamese); oral exams (MAN pp. 9, 11). Count and pass mark: searched alea.gov again (DLI, GDL, FEES, site search); not published. Book keeps "ALEA does not publish".
- Learner at 15: supervisor parent, guardian, licensed 21+, or licensed or certified instructor, front seat (MAN p. 84; GDL). Note MAN p. 9 lists only licensed 21+ or instructor; the book follows MAN p. 84 and GDL. After 16: any licensed driver (MAN pp. 9 to 10), kept with "according to the ALEA manual".
- Six-month hold under 18 (MAN p. 10; GDL; STAT). 50 hours on DL-31 certified by parent, guardian, grandparent or licensed/certified instructor, or SDE driver ed (GDL; STAT (b)). No night-hour count.
- Stage II limits: midnight to 6 a.m.; one non-family passenger (parent, guardian, family, licensed 21+ excluded); no handheld device (MAN p. 84; GDL; STAT (c)).
- Second moving violation or listed offenses or 4+ points: automatic 60 days (MAN p. 84; GDL; STAT (e)).
- Stage III: 17 with six months on Stage II and no moving violation conviction in prior six months, or 18; exemptions married/head of household, relieved of minority (STAT (f), (g); MAN p. 84). Fee $31.25 (GDL).
- School enrollment: under 19 needs DL-1/93 or graduation (MAN p. 8; FEES). HB33 (2026) passed the House only and did not become law; ALEA says no change to 32-6-7.4 (FOX10). Current rule kept.
- New residents: 30 days on prior state license; out-of-state license expired no more than one year, no examination; transfer at ALEA office (MAN p. 5; DLI; FEES).
- Fees: test $5, learner $36.25, license $36.25, 4 percent card fee, no checks, counties may charge more (FEES; GDL; MAN p. 11).
