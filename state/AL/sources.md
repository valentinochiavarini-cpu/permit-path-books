# Alabama sources

All checked 2026-10-28. "MAN" is the **Alabama Driver Manual** (ALEA Driver License Division, edition dated November 2024, 92 PDF pages), read in full from the Drive copy (file ID 1Y1wFmiCoA4ozZjM_dZwMY-JP4A0uxlsJ). No newer edition was found. Page numbers are the manual's printed page numbers (PDF page minus 2). The point schedule on p. 19 is partly garbled in the text export; the suspension table and the point values used here are legible.

Short names:
- **GDL**: ALEA, Graduated Driver License, https://www.alea.gov/dps/driver-license/license-and-id-cards/graduated-driver-license (Act 2010-735; DL-31 form)
- **FEES**: ALEA, Document Requirements and Fees, https://alea.gov/dps/driver-license/license-and-id-cards
- **DLI**: ALEA, Driver License Information, https://www.alea.gov/dps/driver-license/driver-license-information
- **RT**: ALEA, Road Test Study Guide, https://ALEA.gov/sites/default/files/2018-10/road_test_study_guide.pdf
- **MO**: ALEA, Alabama Move Over Act (Code of Ala. 32-5A-58.2, last amended by Act 2024-295), https://www.alea.gov/dps/highway-patrol/alabama-move-over-act
- **ALEA19**: ALEA news release, Three Traffic Laws Effective Sunday, Sept. 1 (2019), https://alea.gov/news/three-traffic-laws-effective-sunday-sept-1
- **FL s**: Code of Alabama section s on FindLaw, https://codes.findlaw.com/al/title-32-motor-vehicles-and-traffic/ (FindLaw notes it may lag the official code; law.justia.com and alison.legislature.state.al.us could not be fetched in this session)
- **HF**: Alabama hands-free law: Wetumpka Herald, "Alabama enforces amendment to hands-free driving law", https://www.thewetumpkaherald.com/alabama-enforces-amendment-to-hands-free-driving-law/article_b18e4726-04ce-583e-82fb-0a58b29529d0.html; WBRC, https://www.wbrc.com/2024/03/12/hands-free-law-take-effect-alabama-this-june (news, secondary to MAN p. 16)
- **WZ**: WBRC, "New work zone safety law goes into effect July 1" (2021), https://www.wbrc.com/2021/06/24/new-work-zone-safety-law-goes-into-effect-july-1 (news; statute 32-5A-176.1 text not fetched)
- **UA**: University of Alabama, State and City Alcohol Penalties (2019), https://biennial.sa.ua.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/66/2019/07/State-and-City-Alcohol-Penalties.pdf (secondary, Title 28 penalties)
- **ISSS**: University of Alabama ISSS, Transportation, Driving and Driver's Licenses, https://international.ua.edu/isss/life-in-tuscaloosa/transportation-driving/ (secondary, foreign license holders)
