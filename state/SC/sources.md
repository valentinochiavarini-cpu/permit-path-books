# South Carolina sources

All checked 2026-10-28. "MAN" is the **South Carolina Driver's License Manual** (SCDMV, AAMVA model based), Drive copy file ID 1rArPsnTdvIbxpAViOFvcn03tYTQvke-2. The connector export ends at Sec. 7, p. 7-5 (Diverging Diamond Interchange): Sections 1 to 6 and part of 7 were read in full; Sections 7 (school buses onward) to 12 were not available in it. Page numbers are the manual's printed numbers (for example 1-4).

The SCDMV site links a newer manual (dmv.sc.gov/sites/scdmv/files/2026-04/Driver's Manual.pdf; an earlier 2026-01 file also exists). Neither could be read here (binary or permission timeout), so it is not known what changed. Gaps were filled from statute and from **MAN-OLD**, an earlier SCDMV edition of the same manual hosted by SCDPS (scdps.sc.gov/sites/scdps/files/Documents/ohsjp/Driver%20Manual.pdf), and **MAN-COASTAL**, an older edition hosted by Coastal Carolina University (coastal.edu); each fact taken from them is marked and was matched to statute where possible.

- **SCC s**: S.C. Code of Laws section s. Read at https://law.justia.com/codes/south-carolina/ (2025 code) unless marked **(SH)**, read at https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t56c005.php (current; that page is cut off after 56-5-1890 by the fetch tool).

## Knowledge test format

Not published. BP, TEST, TEEN, RT, MOVE and MAN Sec. 1 (pp. 1-10 to 1-13) give no question count, pass mark, sign section or retest wait for the knowledge test. MAN Sec. 12 (practice test) was not in the export. The SCDMV practice exam page (apps.sc.gov/DLPracticeExam/regdl/StartExam) and the manual revision history PDF could not be opened (permission timeout). Third-party prep sites commonly say 30 questions and 80 percent; not used (not official, and the brief forbids reading prep sites). MAN-COASTAL says knowledge and skills tests may be taken only once a day; not printed (old edition).

## Law changes checked (2025 and 2026)

- 2025 Act No. 40 (H.3276), Hands-Free and Distracted Driving Act, SCC 56-5-3890, effective 2025-09-01; warnings only for 180 days; tickets from 2026-02-28. In the book.
- 2025 Act No. 38 (H.3127), failure to stop for a law enforcement signal (SCC 56-5-750), effective 2026-05-12. In the police_stop box.
- 2024 Act No. 146 (H.4673), beginner's permits (SCC 56-1-50), effective 2024-11-13: 180-day hold for anyone 15+ never licensed; 30 days at 18+. In Chapter 1.
- 2024 Act No. 130 (H.3518), implied consent changes (SCC 56-5-2951), effective 2024-05-19; 2023 Act No. 55 (S.36), ignition interlock for first DUI (SCC 56-5-2990), effective 2024-05-19. In the boxes.
- Pending, not law: H.4304 (2025-26), school bus camera civil penalties; in House committee.
