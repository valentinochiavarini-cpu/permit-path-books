## Core sentences checked against SC law

No core sentence contradicts South Carolina law. Notes:
- ch04 4.7 figure caption and text mention a "divided highway". SC has no divided-highway rule; the box gives the 4-lane rule and the caption defers to the box. Not a contradiction.
- ch10 10.9 says "Flashing red and blue lights" mean pull over; the SC box says police use blue. Compatible (56-5-170 allows red or blue).
- ch10 10.9 says reach for documents only when asked; the SC box says "have them ready to show". Compatible.
- ch07 7.2 says signal a lane change "at least as far ahead as for a turn"; general advice, not a legal statement; no conflict with the edited signal_distance box.
