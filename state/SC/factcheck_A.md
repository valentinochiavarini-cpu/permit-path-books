## Text changes in ch01.md
1. Sec. 1.2: "If you are not a U.S. citizen" changed to "If you are a lawfully present non-U.S. citizen". SC licenses only lawfully present non-citizens; the SCDMV checklist is titled "MV-94 Lawfully Present Non-US Citizens' Checklist" (BP link, dmv.sc.gov/sites/scdmv/files/2026-06/MV-94...).
2. Sec. 1.6: "For the last two options with paperwork, the SCDMV needs a copy" changed to "For the Social Services approval and the notarized permission, the SCDMV needs a copy." The old wording could be read as the last two list items (in loco parentis and notarized); MAN26 p. 1-5 requires copies for the DSS approval and the notarized permission.

Word count after edits: 2,252 (wc, including markup). No dashes, no years.
