## Checked and confirmed (no change)

- Written exam: at least 35 questions, 80 percent (AC 1030.80(b)); sign reading required (1030.80(g), 6-109(b)); oral exam for illiterate applicants (1030.80(e)); oral exams Monday to Thursday in person, interpreter for deaf or hard of hearing on request (RR p. 15).
- Attempts: three within one year (RR p. 13; AC 1030.86 counts from the first attempt); same-day retest allowed during business hours unless the applicant was a danger on the first try, no third road test the same day (AC 1030.86), so "sometimes possible" stands.
- Permit at 15 with enrollment or start within 30 days; 17 years 3 months without driver ed; valid 24 months; results valid until permit expires (RR p. 16).
- 9 consecutive months, 50 hours with 10 at night, no infractions, alcohol convictions or supervisions (RR pp. 16, 18; AC 1030.5(c)).
- Supervisor 21+, valid license, 1 year experience, front passenger seat (RR p. 16).
- Night limits Sun to Thu 10 p.m. to 6 a.m., Fri and Sat 11 p.m. to 6 a.m., permit and initial phases (RR p. 18).
- Passengers: permit one front plus belts; initial phase first 12 months or until 18 one under 20 except siblings, stepsiblings, children, stepchildren (RR p. 18).
- Adults 18 to 20: 6-hour Adult Driver Education Course, SOS-certified provider, classroom or online (RR pp. 4, 18; AC 1030.5(d)).
- Fees $20, $5, $30, $5; no extra fee if licensed before permit expires (RR pp. 11, 17).
- Standard license replaced TVDL from July 1, 2024 (RR pp. 4, 6, 8, 12); "Federal Limits Apply" marking (AC 1030.5(h)). Book wording is neutral.
- Vision 20/40, 20/41 to 20/70 daylight only, 140 degrees, Vision Specialist Report within 6 months (RR p. 13).
- Road test: 36 points (AC 1030.85(g)); immediate disqualification for a ticket, dangerous action, refusal (1030.85(h)); tasks and car rules (RR pp. 14 to 15).
- Under-21 penalties and parental cancellation (RR pp. 19 to 20).
- New residents: 90 days or before license expires (RR p. 5).

## New resident verdict

Not confirmed by the Secretary of State (ilsos.gov New Residents and FAQ pages blocked). Evidence:

- Statute: 6-109(a) requires the full examination only for applicants never licensed in Illinois or any other state or country, and for renewals expired over one year; the Secretary "may" examine any other applicant. So the law neither requires nor forbids testing a new resident with a valid license; SOS practice decides.
- Village of Bartlett (government page, https://www.bartlettil.gov/services/utilities-resident-services/driver-s-licenses): out-of-state residents get an Illinois license "by taking a written examination and a vision test".
- I Drive Safely (https://www.idrivesafely.com/drivers-ed/illinois/drivers-license-adult/), driving-tests.org (https://driving-tests.org/illinois/drivers-license-guide/) and Finhabits (2026-03-10) say the same: vision and written required, driving exam possible or not typical.
- Only low-quality pages (mydmvappointment.com, catalystlegal.org) say both tests are waived.
- RR p. 13: a crash or moving violation on the record may require the written and/or driving exam.

Verdict: the written exam is most likely **not** waived for a valid out-of-state license in Illinois (vision plus written; driving exam usually not required). The book now says to plan on the written exam. This contradicts `docs/decisions.md` (Positioning: "14 of 15 states waive the test ... California is the exception") and `docs/states.md` (IL new residents "unverified"); the owner should confirm on ilsos.gov New Residents before relying on either.
