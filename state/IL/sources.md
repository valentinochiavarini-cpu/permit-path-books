This is the newest edition (the online file at ilsos.gov is titled "2026 Illinois Rules of the Road"). The Drive text stops at RR page 77 (start of Chapter 10), so pages 78 to 106 (following distance, weather, equipment, insurance, ID chart) were filled from statutes and other primary sources below. Page numbers are RR page numbers.

## Law changes checked (2025 and 2026)

- HB 1226 (eff. 2026-07-01): mandatory renewal road test age raised from 79 to 87; family members may report unsafe drivers. Affects renewals only, not new drivers (ILAO; RR p. 14).
- Scott's Law amended by P.A. 103-711 (eff. 2025-01-01): emergency scene definition and speed reduction wording.
- Headlamp sections amended by P.A. 103-706 (eff. 2025-01-01); dimming distances unchanged.
- 11-703 amended by P.A. 104-260 (eff. 2025-08-15); whether the no-passing-zone bicycle rule is new in that act was not confirmed, so the book does not date it.
- HB 66 (raise insurance minimums to 50/100/40) did not pass.
- No 2025 or 2026 change found to the permit, GDL or written exam rules; RR (July 2026) matches ILCS 6-107 (latest P.A. 103-154, 2023).
