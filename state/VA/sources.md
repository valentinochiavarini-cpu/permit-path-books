## Law changes checked (2025 and 2026 sessions)
- July 1, 2025: seat belts required for all adults in rear seats (secondary enforcement). Used.
- 2026 HB 1224: first-time applicants 18 to 20 need 90-day hold plus driver ed; DMV applies it from January 1, 2027. Used, without a year in the book.
- 2026 HB 320 (Code 46.2-818.3): live streaming while driving banned. Used.
- 2026 SB 84: localities may use cameras for stop sign and crosswalk violations in school zones. Not used (local option, details not verified).
- 2026 HB 561: wider ignition interlock and ASAP use. Not used (details not verified).
- Intelligent speed assistance devices for some reckless drivers (passed 2025, effective 2026). Not used.

## Unverified or left out
- Online knowledge exam at home: none found; only Remote Testing in Schools. Teen online driver ed classroom exam is a separate test.
- Current on-road road test scoring sheet: DMV page gives no scoring details; the testing guide describes the retired closed course.
